## Verification
- Verified with oracles: video-metadata-inspector, video-muter (ffprobe and ffmpeg decode on MP4 and WebM test files); font tools (WOFF round trip table-by-table, fvar axes against an independent struct parse, fc-scan charset); palette-exporter ASE (parsed back with Python struct, GPL text), color maths (CIE Lab red = 53.24/80.09/67.20, OKLab and contrast against css-color-formats and contrast-checker on this site, Krystek Planckian locus x,y at 6500 K and 2856 K against published values); business-card PDF (pdfinfo boxes, pdftoppm render); subtitle-timing-sync (hand-checked linear map); zip writer (Python zipfile testzip).
- Run in Chromium via DevTools: video-trimmer, speed changer, subtitle-burner (visual), merger, reverser, looper, pip, music mixer, frame grabber, contact sheet, audio extractor, muter, rotator, cropper, resizer, compressor, watermarker, format converter, frame rate converter; font inspector and previewer; all image-output tools (screenshot); styled QR decoded with the site's own qr-code-reader (5 style combinations).
- Not verified: Firefox and Safari behaviour (MediaRecorder MP4 and WebM support differ); camera tools (stop-motion-maker, teleprompter-recorder, webcam-color-sampler) only rendered, not used with a camera; video-side-by-side-player and color-hue-quiz not exercised by clicking; ASE not opened in Adobe software; business card not printed; MP4 output of format converter not tried.
