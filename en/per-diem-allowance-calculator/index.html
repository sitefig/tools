Per Diem Allowance Calculator (Business Trip Daily Allowance)

Trip allowance from 24-hour blocks, a part-day band and meal deductions.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Daily allowances depend on destination and trip length. Enter your own full-day rate, part-day rate and the cut for each meal provided.

The model is deliberately simple: 24-hour blocks plus one part-day band. Official systems differ, so no rates are built in.

How to use it

  1. Enter departure and return times.
  2. Enter day rates and the part-day threshold.
  3. Enter meals provided and cuts.

Frequently asked questions

Which rates apply?

Those from your tax authority or employer for the destination.

How are partial days handled?

The remainder over the threshold gets the part-day rate.

Does it handle time zones?

No. Use local clock times consistently.
