Test Email Address Generator

Creates test addresses that all deliver to one real inbox, or addresses on reserved domains that can never deliver.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Signup flows need many different addresses. Plus addressing (name+tag@domain) sends mail to name@domain on many providers, so one inbox can serve many test accounts. Gmail also ignores dots in the name part.

Not every provider supports plus tags, and some forms reject the plus sign, so test with your own mail system. For tests that must never reach a person, use the reserved domains example.com, example.org and example.net (RFC 2606) and the reserved top-level names .test, .example and .invalid.

How to use it

  1. Enter your base address.
  2. Pick numbered, random or your own tag words, Gmail dots or reserved domains.
  3. Set the count.
  4. Copy the list.

Frequently asked questions

Does every provider support plus tags?

No. Gmail, Outlook.com, iCloud and Fastmail do. Many company systems do not.

Do dots work everywhere?

Only on Gmail and googlemail.com. Elsewhere they make a different address.

Which domains are safe for fake data?

example.com, example.org, example.net and the .test, .example and .invalid names are reserved and never get real mail.

Will it send email?

No. It only writes the addresses.
