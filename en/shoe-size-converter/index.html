Shoe Size Converter From Foot Length (EU, UK, US Men and Women)

Converts between foot length, EU, UK and US adult shoe sizes using the published formulas.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Shoe size systems measure different things. UK and US sizes count barleycorns of the last, and EU sizes count Paris points. The ISO conversion uses foot length: EU = 0.15 x length in mm + 2, UK = 3 x length / 25.4 - 23.

US men is one above UK in these formulas, and US women is another 1.5 above, which is the common scale. Brands cut shoes differently, so treat the output as a guide and check the maker's chart. Children's sizes are not covered.

How to use it

  1. Choose what you know: foot length or a size system.
  2. Enter the value.
  3. Read all systems and the nearest half sizes.

Frequently asked questions

How do I measure foot length?

Stand on paper, mark heel and longest toe, and measure the distance. Do it in the evening.

Why do brands differ?

Lasts and fit differ, so the same number fits differently.

Are children's sizes included?

No. They use other zero points.
