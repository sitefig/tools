PDF Dark Mode Converter (Invert for Night Reading)

Read a white PDF comfortably at night.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Pages are rendered with the built-in PDF renderer, the pixels are inverted (optionally rotating hues by 180 degrees so colours stay recognisable, using the same matrix as CSS invert plus hue-rotate) and the pages are rebuilt into a new PDF. Resolution and JPEG quality control size.

Because pages are stored as images, text can no longer be selected or searched and files may be larger. Keep the original for anything that needs real text. Images in the PDF are inverted too.

The file is read in your browser and is not uploaded anywhere.

How to use it

  1. Choose the PDF and the style.
  2. Pick pages, resolution and quality.
  3. Download the dark copy.
  4. Keep the original file.

Frequently asked questions

Does it keep the text?

No, the result is page images.

Why is it big?

Higher DPI makes bigger images; try 90 to 110 DPI.

Does it change colours?

"Invert, keep hues" tries to keep colours recognisable; plain invert gives the negative.

Is my data sent anywhere?

The file is read in your browser and is not uploaded anywhere.

Tools often used together

  • PDF to Image (PNG, JPEG, WebP)Render PDF pages to PNG, JPEG or WebP images with a built-in renderer (nothing is uploaded). Text, vector graphics, gradients, patterns, transparency and images are supported; JBIG2/JPEG2000 images show as grey boxes.
  • Images to PDFCombine images into one PDF, one per page. JPEGs are embedded untouched; PNG/WebP/GIF are re-encoded (PNG transparency can be kept losslessly).
  • PDF Compare (Visual Difference Between Two PDFs)Compare two versions of a PDF page by page: pages are rendered and differences are highlighted in red, with the percentage changed and where. Works for scans, text and layout.
  • PDF Redaction (Black Out Areas Permanently)Black out sensitive areas of a PDF by page and position (percent of the page). Pages are flattened to images, so the covered text is really gone, not just hidden under a box.
  • PDF InfoInspect a plain, non-encrypted PDF: version, page count, Info fields, producer, encryption, object streams. Best effort (regex based; no repair of broken files).
  • Merge PDFsCombine several PDFs into one, in the order selected. Works on existing PDFs without re-rendering; bookmarks and form fields are dropped.
