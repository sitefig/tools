Text Similarity & Duplicate Content Checker

Check how similar two pieces of text are, find the phrases they share, and judge whether one is a near-copy of the other.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Both texts are lowercased and split into words. The tool reports the Jaccard similarity of word k-shingles (3 by default), how much of each text appears in the other, the cosine similarity of word counts and any shared runs of five or more words. A verdict from "largely different" to "near-duplicate" summarises the result.

It compares only what you paste, so it cannot detect plagiarism from the web. It is useful for comparing product descriptions, rewriting drafts, localised versions or old and new page copy.

No data is sent anywhere: the work happens in your browser, so you can use it offline once the page has loaded.

How to use it

  1. Paste text A and text B.
  2. Adjust the shingle size if you want a stricter or looser comparison.
  3. Read the percentages and shared phrases.
  4. Rewrite sections that are too close to a source.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a plagiarism checker?

Only for two texts you provide. It does not search any database or website.

What is a shingle?

A run of k consecutive words. Shared shingles measure copied phrasing better than shared single words.

How similar is too similar?

Above about 60% shingle overlap is a near-duplicate; the right threshold depends on your use.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything is processed in your browser, and the page makes no network requests with your input.
