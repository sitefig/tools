Cooking Converter: Cups, Tablespoons and Grams by Ingredient

Converts between cups, spoons, millilitres, grams and ounces for 17 baking ingredients, using typical grams per US cup.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

A cup of flour and a cup of sugar weigh different amounts, so cups to grams depends on the ingredient. Flour is about 120 g a cup, granulated sugar about 198 g.

Values come from the King Arthur Baking ingredient weight chart (read 2026-10-23). Scooping and packing change real weight, so use a scale for baking.

How to use it

  1. Choose the ingredient.
  2. Enter the amount and its unit.
  3. Read the other measures.

Frequently asked questions

How many grams is a cup of flour?

120 g here, from the King Arthur chart. Other sources say 125 to 140 g.

Why do results differ from my recipe?

Packing, sifting and brand change the weight. Trust the recipe weight first.

Are spoons US spoons?

Yes, a US tablespoon is 14.79 mL and a teaspoon 4.93 mL.
