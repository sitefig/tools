Video Frame Grabber: Save Still Frames at Chosen Timestamps

Pulls still images out of a video at times you list, or every N seconds, in full resolution as PNG or JPG.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

To get a thumbnail, a screenshot for a bug report or a frame for a storyboard you need an exact moment. Enter timestamps like 1.5, 0:10 or 1:02:03, or set an interval. The player lets you find the time first.

Each frame is saved at the video size. Many frames are also offered as one ZIP. No upload happens.

How to use it

  1. Choose the video and find your moments in the player.
  2. Type the timestamps, or set an interval in seconds.
  3. Pick PNG or JPG and press Process.
  4. Download single frames or the ZIP.

Frequently asked questions

Is it the exact frame?

It is the frame the browser shows at that time, which is within one frame of the request.

PNG or JPG?

PNG is lossless and larger. JPG is smaller.

What is the maximum?

Up to 200 frames in interval mode.

Can I get every frame?

Use a small interval, but memory limits apply for very long videos.
