Percentage Calculator

Pick one of three modes: find X% of Y, find what percentage X is of Y, or work out the percentage change from X to Y. The formula is shown with the result.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Percentages come up in small, awkward moments: a 15% service charge on a bill, a stock that moved from 80 to 100, a test score of 37 out of 52. Most people can do the easy cases in their head and get the rest wrong, usually by dividing the wrong way round. This calculator keeps the three most common questions in one place and prints the formula next to the answer, so you can check the logic as well as the number. It runs entirely in your browser, which means you can paste in sales figures or internal numbers without sending them anywhere. Results keep up to six decimal places, so small ratios stay readable.

How to use it

  1. Choose a mode: X% of Y, X is what % of Y, or Change from X to Y.
  2. Type your two numbers into the X and Y fields.
  3. Read the result and the formula underneath it, then copy the text if you need it.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between percentage change and percentage points?

Percentage change compares two values as a ratio of the starting one. Going from 10% to 15% is a rise of 5 percentage points but a 50% relative increase. This tool calculates the relative change.

What happens if X is zero in change mode?

The calculator shows an error, because a change measured against zero is undefined. In 'X is what % of Y' mode, Y cannot be zero for the same reason.

Why does a decrease use the absolute value of X?

The change is divided by the absolute value of the starting number, so a move from -50 to -25 is reported as an increase. This avoids the sign flipping the meaning of the result.

Is anything I enter stored or sent?

No. The calculation runs in your browser, nothing you type is uploaded, and the page keeps no history, so you can safely use real figures from work.
