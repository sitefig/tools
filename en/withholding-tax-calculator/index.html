Withholding Tax Calculator (Net from Gross, Gross-Up from Net)

Net from gross, or gross from net, at the withholding rate you enter.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Cross-border payments often have tax withheld at source. The net you receive is gross times one minus the rate, so to receive a fixed net you must invoice more.

An optional treaty rate shows the saving when a tax treaty reduces the rate. Whether the payer bears the withholding is a contract matter.

How to use it

  1. Choose net from gross or gross from net.
  2. Enter the amount and rate.
  3. Optionally enter the treaty rate.

Frequently asked questions

Is the gross-up always right?

Only if the payer withholds on the grossed-up sum.

Can I credit the tax?

Often in your own country. Ask your adviser.

Are rates built in?

No.
