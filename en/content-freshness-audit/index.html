Content Freshness Audit (Outdated Years, Versions and Time Phrases)

Find the parts of an article that have aged and get a checklist for refreshing it.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Old content loses rankings and trust quietly. This audit scans the text for years older than a threshold you set, relative-time words ("this year", "recently", "currently"), software versions, pandemic wording, statistics with no date and old copyright years, shows the context of each hit and ends with a refresh checklist covering dates, schema, sources and links.

It is pattern matching: it cannot tell whether a statement is still true, so use the hits as a to-do list and verify each against a current source.

How to use it

  1. Paste the article text or HTML.
  2. Set the current year and the age threshold.
  3. Work through the hits and the checklist.

Frequently asked questions

Does it check facts?

No. It finds places that are likely to be outdated.

Will updating the date help rankings?

Only if the content really changed; fake freshness does not help.

Can it fetch my URL?

No; paste the content.
