Browser Console Log Cleaner: Strip Links and Group Repeats

Strips source links and known noise from copied console output and counts repeats.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Copying the console gives lines with file:line prefixes, VM numbers and dev-server chatter. Cleaning them makes the output fit for a bug report.

The noise list is short and shown in the result: React DevTools hint, source-map warnings, vite, HMR, webpack-dev-server, Violation and third-party cookie notices. Indented stack lines are kept.

How to use it

  1. Copy the console output and paste it.
  2. Choose which cleanups to apply.
  3. Choose how to collapse repeats.
  4. Copy the result.

Frequently asked questions

Will it remove real errors?

No. Only the listed noise patterns and source prefixes are removed.

What is collapse every repeat?

Identical lines are merged into one with a count, even if they were apart.

Does it hide secrets?

No. Use the secret redactor for that.

Is my log uploaded?

No. Everything is processed in your browser and nothing is sent. Logs can hold IP addresses and tokens, so the page does not write them into the address bar.
