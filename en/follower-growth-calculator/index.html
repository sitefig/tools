Follower Growth Rate Calculator with Projection

Compute follower growth in numbers and percent between two counts, per day and per month, and project future counts at the same pace.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Growth numbers mean little without a period. Fifty followers a week is fast for a small account and slow for a large one.

Enter the start count, the current count and the days between. The tool shows the change, the percentage, the per-day and per-month pace and the compound monthly rate, then projects the count at that pace both linearly and compounded. Growth rarely stays steady, so treat the projection as a planning aid.

How to use it

  1. Enter the follower count at the start and now.
  2. Enter the number of days between.
  3. Set how many months to project.
  4. Compare the linear and compound columns.

Frequently asked questions

Linear or compound?

Linear adds the same number each month, compound grows by the same percentage. Compound runs away on long periods.

Is the projection reliable?

No. It assumes the recent pace continues.

Can I use it for subscribers or email lists?

Yes. It works for any count.
