Break-Even Selling Price Calculator (Product, Shipping, Fees, Ads)

Finds the price at which an order covers product cost, packaging, shipping, ads and percentage fees, and the price needed for a profit you choose.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

A price that looks fine can lose money once fees that scale with the price are counted. Fixed costs are simple to add, but a percentage fee is taken from the price itself, so the formula divides by one minus the fee rate rather than adding a percentage on top.

Enter your costs per order and any target profit. The tool shows the break-even price and the price for your target, and checks the result by working the profit back.

How to use it

  1. Enter product, packaging and shipping costs.
  2. Enter ad cost per order, the percentage fees and the fixed fee per order.
  3. Optionally set a target profit per order and read the price.

Frequently asked questions

Why not just add the fee percent to the cost?

Because the fee is a share of the final price. Adding 12 percent of cost undercharges. The tool divides by (1 - fee%) instead.

Should returns be included?

Add an average cost per order into ad cost or product cost if you want to spread it. The tool has no separate returns field.

Is the margin shown on price or cost?

On price. It is profit divided by the selling price.
