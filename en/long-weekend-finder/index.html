Long Weekend and Bridge Day Finder from Your Holiday List

Finds runs of three or more days off created by your holidays and weekends, and shows which one or two leave days would join them.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Planning leave around holidays means looking for a Thursday holiday and taking Friday. Paste your own list of public holidays and this does the search.

It does not know any country's holidays, since that needs an up-to-date source, so you supply the dates. Substitute days are not added.

How to use it

  1. Paste holidays as YYYY-MM-DD with an optional name.
  2. Choose your weekend days and how many leave days you will bridge.
  3. Read the long weekends and bridge options.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I get holiday dates?

From your government or employer calendar. The tool does not fetch them.

Does it cover multi-year lists?

Yes, any years in the list.

What about a different weekend?

Choose Friday and Saturday for countries that use it.
