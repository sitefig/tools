Probability Distribution Calculator and Plot: Normal, Binomial, Poisson

Calculates the probability that a normal, binomial or Poisson variable falls in an interval, with the mean, variance and a shaded plot.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Textbook problems ask for things like the chance of 85 to 115 on a test with mean 100 and standard deviation 15, or of 2 to 6 events when the average is 4.5. Enter the parameters and the interval and the probability is shaded on the plot.

The normal CDF uses a series and continued-fraction form of the error function accurate to about 1e-13. Binomial and Poisson masses use log-factorials so large numbers of trials do not overflow. The results were compared with SciPy for many cases. For discrete distributions both ends of the interval are included.

How to use it

  1. Choose the distribution.
  2. Enter its parameters: mean and standard deviation, or trials and probability, or the rate.
  3. Enter the from and to values, leaving one blank for an open end.
  4. Read the probability and look at the shaded area.

Frequently asked questions

Are the limits inclusive?

For binomial and Poisson yes, for the normal distribution it makes no difference.

How large can the binomial n be?

Up to 100000 trials.

What is the Poisson limit?

Rates up to 5000.

Does it give percentiles?

No, only probabilities of intervals.
