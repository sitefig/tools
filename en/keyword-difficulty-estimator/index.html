Keyword Difficulty Estimator from Competitor Metrics You Enter

Enter the average authority and referring domains of the pages that rank for a keyword and get a simple difficulty score, a label and the gap to your site.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Vendor keyword difficulty scores are black boxes and cost money. If you already collected the numbers from the results, a transparent formula is enough to rank your own list.

Difficulty is 50% of average authority plus 50% of a log-scaled link score (referring domains). The formula is shown under the results. It ignores content quality, search intent, brand strength and SERP features, and it is not comparable with any tool's own score. Use it to sort your list, not to predict rankings.

How to use it

  1. Enter one keyword per line: keyword, average authority, average referring domains and optionally the lowest authority in the top 10.
  2. Set your own site's authority on the same scale.
  3. Read the table from easiest to hardest.
  4. Look for weak spots, where a low-authority page already ranks.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I get the numbers?

From any SEO tool that shows authority and referring domains for the top results. Use the same tool for all keywords.

What is the link cap?

The number of referring domains that counts as a full link score. Raise it for very competitive niches.

Is a high score impossible to beat?

Not always. Intent fit and content quality matter, and small sites rank for hard keywords.

Why a log scale?

The step from 5 to 50 referring domains matters more than from 500 to 550.
