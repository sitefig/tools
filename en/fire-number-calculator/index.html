FIRE Number Calculator (Target Portfolio and Years to Reach It)

FIRE number, progress to date and years left from your savings and expected real return.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

The FIRE number is annual spending divided by a withdrawal rate. At 4% it is 25 times spending. The rate is a rule of thumb from historical market data; longer retirements usually call for lower rates.

The years estimate uses a constant real return (after inflation) and yearly contributions. Real markets are uneven, so treat it as a rough guide and test several return values.

How to use it

  1. Enter annual spending and a withdrawal rate.
  2. Enter your invested amount and yearly savings.
  3. Set a real return and read the years.

Frequently asked questions

Should I use real or nominal returns?

Real returns, after inflation, so that spending stays in today's money.

Is 4% safe?

It is a rule of thumb, not a guarantee. Use a lower rate for longer horizons.

Are taxes included?

No. Taxes and fees are not modelled.
