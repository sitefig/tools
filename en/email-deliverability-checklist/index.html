Email Deliverability Checklist (Authentication, List, Content)

Work through authentication, list hygiene, sending practice and content items, with progress saved in this browser.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Inbox placement depends on many small things done right: authentication records, a clean consented list, steady volume and honest content. A checklist makes gaps visible before a campaign instead of after.

It is a starting list, not a guarantee. Mailbox providers change requirements, so check current Google and Yahoo sender rules. The complaint-rate item reflects Google's guideline of staying below 0.1% and never reaching 0.3%. Progress is stored in your browser and not sent anywhere.

How to use it

  1. Tick the items you have done.
  2. Follow up on the unticked ones, starting with authentication.
  3. Use the record analysers to check SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
  4. Come back before large campaigns.

Frequently asked questions

Is my progress saved?

Yes, in this browser only, using local storage.

Is this enough for inbox placement?

No. It lowers risk but cannot guarantee delivery.

Where is the one-click unsubscribe rule from?

Google and Yahoo ask bulk senders for it. Check their current pages.

How do I reset?

Press Reset all.

Tools often used together

  • DMARC Record Analyzer (Explain a TXT Value)Paste a DMARC TXT record and get every tag explained: policy, subdomain policy, percentage, alignment, report addresses, plus warnings such as p=none or pct below 100.
  • SPF Record GeneratorCompose an SPF TXT record from mechanisms, count DNS lookups against the 10-lookup limit and check the length.
  • DKIM Record Analyzer (Key Type and RSA Key Length)Paste a DKIM TXT record and read its tags, find the RSA key size or Ed25519 key, spot test mode and revoked keys, and get a note when the key is shorter than 2048 bits.
  • Email Header Analyzer (Received Path, SPF, DKIM, DMARC)Paste full email headers to trace the route (each Received hop with delay), read SPF, DKIM and DMARC results, check From / Return-Path alignment and spot spoofing signs.
  • Spam Trigger Word Checker (Subject and Body)Highlight words and phrases in an email subject and body that are often cited as spam triggers, with a count and a list. A word list heuristic, not a real spam filter.
  • GDPR Compliance ChecklistInteractive GDPR self-assessment checklist. Progress is saved in your browser (localStorage) only.
