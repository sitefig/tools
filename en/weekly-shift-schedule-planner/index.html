Weekly Shift Schedule Planner with Hour Totals

Turns a text list of shifts into a weekly rota table with paid hours per person, breaks and daily headcount.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Write one line per person, for example Ana: Mon 9-17 (30), Tue 9-17 (30), Wed off. The break in brackets is unpaid minutes. The tool builds the grid, adds up each person's hours and counts how many people are on shift each day.

Overnight shifts such as 22-6 are supported and counted on the day they start. Hours can be shown as decimals or as hours and minutes. The tool does not check legal limits such as maximum hours or rest periods; those depend on your country and contract. Print the table in landscape.

How to use it

  1. Write one person per line with Day start-end entries.
  2. Add an unpaid break in brackets if needed.
  3. Choose decimal or hours:minutes.
  4. Print the rota.

Frequently asked questions

What time formats work?

9-17, 09:00-17:30 and 22-6.

Does it check labour law?

No.

How are overnight shifts counted?

On the day they start.

Can I assign by dragging?

No, edit the text.
