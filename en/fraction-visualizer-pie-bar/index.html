Fraction Visualizer: Pie and Bar Models with Comparison

Draws each fraction as a pie and a bar, converts it to lowest terms, mixed number and decimal, and compares two fractions.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Fractions make more sense when you can see them. Type 3/8 and see 3 of 8 slices shaded; type 3/8, 1/2 and the tool draws both and explains the comparison by cross-multiplying.

Mixed numbers like 1 3/4 are drawn as full circles plus a part. Denominators up to 24 and values up to 4 are supported to keep the drawings readable. The text lines give the lowest terms, the mixed number and the exact decimal.

How to use it

  1. Type one fraction, or several separated by commas.
  2. Use 3/8 or 1 3/4 for mixed numbers.
  3. With two fractions the comparison is added.
  4. Read the lines under the drawing.

Frequently asked questions

What is the largest denominator?

24.

Can I use negatives?

Yes, but the picture shows the size of the fraction.

How are two fractions compared?

By cross-multiplication, shown step by step.

Can I print it?

Yes, with your browser print function.
