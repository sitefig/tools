Sticky Notes Board Saved in Your Browser

A board of colored sticky notes that you type on and reorder with buttons, kept in this browser and also usable without storage.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Short reminders fit on small notes. Add a note, type, choose yellow, pink, blue or green, and move it earlier or later in the board.

Notes are kept in this browser only. They are ordered with buttons rather than dragged to any spot, which keeps the board usable with a keyboard and on touch screens. If storage is blocked the board still works but is forgotten when you close the page.

How to use it

  1. Press Add a note and type.
  2. Choose a color for each note.
  3. Use the < and > buttons to move a note.
  4. Delete notes you no longer need.

Frequently asked questions

Can I drag notes anywhere?

No, they are ordered with buttons.

Where are the notes stored?

In this browser on this device.

Can I export them?

Select the text and copy it.

Does it sync?

No.
