Subtitle Timing Shifter and Drift Fixer for SRT

Shifts every subtitle cue by a fixed number of seconds, or rescales all times with two reference points to fix gradual drift.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Subtitles that start in sync but drift later usually come from a frame rate mismatch, for example 23.976 against 25 fps. Two reference points give a straight line: new time equals a times old time plus b. The tool solves that line and applies it to every cue.

For a constant delay use the offset mode. Cues that would end before zero are dropped. The converter on this site shifts by milliseconds too, but has no stretch mode.

How to use it

  1. Paste your SRT.
  2. Pick Shift for a constant delay, or Stretch for drift.
  3. For Stretch, enter two times where the subtitle is now and when it should be.
  4. Copy the corrected SRT.

Frequently asked questions

How do I pick the reference points?

Choose the first and the last line of dialogue. Note when each is heard in the video and its current cue time.

What time formats work?

Seconds, m:ss or h:mm:ss, with decimals.

Does it change the text?

No. Only the times. Cues are renumbered.

What about VTT?
