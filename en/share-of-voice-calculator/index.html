Share of Voice Calculator (Brand vs Competitors)

Enter a value per brand to get each brand's share and a bar chart.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Share of voice divides your number by the total of all listed brands. It works for any single metric, such as mentions, ad spend or impressions.

The share depends on which competitors you include, so keep the set stable between periods. Use the same metric and period for every brand.

How to use it

  1. Enter one line per brand as Name: value.
  2. Give your own brand name to mark it.
  3. Read the shares.
  4. Repeat each period with the same brands.

Frequently asked questions

Which metric should I use?

Any, if it is the same for all brands.

Why did my share drop?

Maybe a new competitor was added.

Does it measure quality?

No, only volume.

Is data stored?

No.
