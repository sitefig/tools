Sight Reading Trainer: Name the Note on the Staff

Shows random notes on a treble or bass staff for you to name, with an optional time limit and a summary of your speed and mistakes.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Reading music quickly means knowing the lines and spaces without counting. A staff is drawn with one note at a time, and you answer with the letter, by button or by pressing A to G.

A round has 20 notes. The summary gives your score, your average answer time and the notes you missed. The picture has a text description and the buttons are real controls, so the trainer can be used with a keyboard. Range is from two ledger lines below the staff to one above it. Nothing is stored.

How to use it

  1. Choose the clef and a time limit.
  2. Press Start 20 notes.
  3. Name each note with a button or key A to G.
  4. Read the summary.

Frequently asked questions

Does it use sharps and flats?

No, only natural notes.

How does the time limit work?

If you do not answer in time the note counts as missed.

Is it for screen reader users?

The staff has a text label but the exercise itself is visual.

Is the clef symbol drawn?

The clef is named in text; the symbol is not drawn.
