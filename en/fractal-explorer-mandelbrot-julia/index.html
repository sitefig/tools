Fractal Explorer: Mandelbrot, Julia and Burning Ship Sets

Renders the Mandelbrot, Julia or Burning Ship fractal at a center point and zoom level you choose, with a smooth color palette.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Zoom far enough into the Mandelbrot set and new shapes keep appearing. This page renders it for any center and zoom, with up to 5000 iterations, and the Julia and Burning Ship variants too.

Colors use a smooth escape-time value so there are no hard bands. Instead of click-and-drag zoom, you type the center and the zoom factor, which also works with a keyboard; try centers like -0.7436 + 0.1318i at a zoom of 100 or more. Rendering happens in your browser. Past about a zoom of 1e13, double precision runs out of digits and the image turns blocky.

How to use it

  1. Choose the set and enter the center and zoom.
  2. Raise the iteration count for deep zooms.
  3. Pick a palette and image width.
  4. Save the image from the result.

Frequently asked questions

Why does it get blocky when I zoom in?

Double-precision numbers have about 15 digits, which limits the zoom.

What are the Julia constants?

The c value that defines the Julia set. Try -0.8 + 0.156i.

Is it slow?

Large images with many iterations take a few seconds.

Can I click to zoom?

No. Enter values instead.
