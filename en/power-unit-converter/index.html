Power Converter: Watts, Kilowatts and Horsepower

Converts between watts, kilowatts, megawatts, mechanical horsepower, metric horsepower (PS), BTU per hour, kcal per hour and tons of refrigeration.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Car, motor and heater ratings come in different units, and there is more than one horsepower. Mechanical horsepower is 745.7 W, metric horsepower is 735.5 W.

Enter one value and see all the others. Both horsepowers are shown so you can tell which one a spec sheet uses.

How to use it

  1. Type the power value.
  2. Choose its unit.
  3. Read the equivalents below.

Frequently asked questions

Which horsepower should I use?

Use mechanical hp for US and UK ratings and PS (metric hp) for most European ones. Check the label.

How many watts is one BTU/h?

About 0.293 W, using the International Table BTU.

What is a ton of refrigeration?

12,000 BTU/h, about 3.517 kW, used for air conditioning capacity.
