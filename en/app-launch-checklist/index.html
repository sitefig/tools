App Launch Checklist

A pre-launch checklist for mobile and web apps, grouped into product, store listing, legal and privacy, and growth. Tick items off, see your progress and copy the list as Markdown.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Launches go wrong on small things: screenshots in the wrong size, a privacy policy URL that returns 404, a crash reporter nobody switched on, support email nobody reads. Each is easy to fix a week early and painful on launch day.

This checklist lists around twenty tasks across four groups, so a team can walk through them together. Your ticks are saved in this browser, so you can come back tomorrow, and a counter shows how far along you are. "Copy as Markdown" gives you the list with checkboxes for an issue tracker or wiki. Nothing is sent anywhere; the list is general, so add anything your store or industry requires.

How to use it

  1. Open the checklist and read through the four groups: Product, Store listing, Legal and privacy, Growth.
  2. Tick each task when it is done. The progress counter at the top updates.
  3. Come back later in the same browser to continue, since ticks are saved automatically.
  4. Use "Copy as Markdown" to paste the list into an issue, or Reset to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress stored?

In your browser's local storage on this device. It is not uploaded, and it will not appear on another device or browser. Clearing site data, or using a private window, removes it.

Is this checklist complete?

No. It covers the common items, but each app store and market has its own rules, for example age ratings or data-safety forms. Treat it as a base to extend.

Can I add my own tasks?

Not in the page. Use "Copy as Markdown" to take the list into your tracker, where you can add tasks of your own.

Does this give legal advice?

No. The legal items remind you to publish terms and a privacy policy and to check consent flows. Have a qualified person review what applies to your product.
