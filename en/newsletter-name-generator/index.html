Newsletter Name Generator (from Topic and Audience)

Enter topics, an audience and a rhythm to get dozens of name ideas.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Newsletter names tend to follow a few patterns that readers recognise at a glance: a rhythm word, a role word like Digest or Brief, and a topic. Trying them all quickly gives you a shortlist.

It is template based, not creative, so expect to adjust. It cannot check availability: search the name, the domain and your email platform before you commit.

How to use it

  1. Enter one or more topics.
  2. Add an audience and pick a rhythm.
  3. Scan the list and shortlist favourites.
  4. Check domain and search availability.

Frequently asked questions

Does it use AI?

No, only templates.

Can I use several topics?

Yes, one per line.

Is the name checked for trademarks?

No, always check yourself.

Is my input stored?

No.
