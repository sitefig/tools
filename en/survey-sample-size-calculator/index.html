Survey Sample Size Calculator (Confidence and Margin of Error)

Choose confidence level, margin of error and population to get the minimum number of responses.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Use the standard formula n0 = z squared times p(1-p) divided by e squared, then adjust for a small population. With 50% expected proportion you get the safest, largest number.

This assumes a random sample of completed answers. Divide by your expected response rate to know how many to invite. Self-selected surveys have bias that no sample size fixes. The z values are the standard normal values for each level.

How to use it

  1. Enter the population or 0 for very large.
  2. Pick confidence and margin of error.
  3. Keep 50% unless you know better.
  4. Read the responses needed.

Frequently asked questions

Why 385?

At 95% confidence and a 5% margin with a large population, the formula gives 385.

Does population matter?

Only when the sample is a sizeable part of it.

What is p?

Your expected proportion; 50 gives the largest sample.

Is data stored?

No.
