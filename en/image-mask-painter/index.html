Image Mask Painter (Paint Transparency, Export PNG)

Paint areas of a picture transparent and save a PNG.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Choose a picture, then paint with a brush to make parts transparent, or switch to restore. You can use a pointer, or the keyboard: focus the picture, press Space to put the brush down or lift it, and move with the arrow keys. A rectangle form can clear a box or everything outside it, and there is undo.

The canvas is limited to 2400 pixels on the long side to keep the page fast. The edges are hard, so it is best for simple cut-outs.

How to use it

  1. Choose a picture.
  2. Set the brush size and mode.
  3. Paint, or use the rectangle form.
  4. Press Save PNG and download.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use it without a mouse?

Yes. Use the arrow keys and Space, or the rectangle form.

Does it find the subject for me?

No. It is manual. There is a separate tool for plain backgrounds.

How many undo steps?

Eight.

Is anything uploaded?

No. The work happens in your browser and the page makes no network request with your input.
