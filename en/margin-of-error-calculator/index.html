Survey Margin of Error Calculator

Enter sample size, your result and confidence level to see the plus or minus range.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

A result of 56% from 400 people is not exactly 56%: at 95% confidence it is about 51% to 61%. The margin shows how far a result could be from the true value due to sampling alone.

It uses the normal approximation and an optional finite population correction. It is rough near 0% or 100% and for small samples, and does not cover bias or poor wording.

How to use it

  1. Enter the number of responses.
  2. Enter the result percentage and confidence level.
  3. Add the population if it is small.
  4. Read the margin and the range.

Frequently asked questions

Does it cover bias?

No, only random sampling error.

What is the worst case?

The margin when the result is 50%.

Which confidence level?

95% is common.

Is data stored?

No.
