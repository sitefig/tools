Video Muter: Remove the Audio Track Without Re-encoding

Removes the audio track from MP4, MOV or WebM files by editing the container in place. The video stream is not re-encoded.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

For MP4 and MOV the audio track box is renamed to an empty box of the same size, so every offset stays valid and the picture data is unchanged. For WebM the audio track entry is blanked in the same way. This is instant and lossless.

The audio bytes may remain unused inside the file, so it does not shrink. Fragmented files and other formats fall back to a re-encode without audio.

How to use it

  1. Choose the video.
  2. Press Process.
  3. Check that the result has no sound.
  4. Download the muted file.

Frequently asked questions

Is it really lossless?

Yes for MP4, MOV and WebM files that are not fragmented. The video bytes are copied as they are.

Is the audio really gone?

Players no longer see the track. The old bytes may still be inside the file, so use a full re-encode if you need them removed.

Why did it re-encode my file?

The format could not be edited in place, for example a fragmented MP4. The fallback records the video without sound.

Does ffmpeg complain about a WebM?

It may print a warning about an unknown track number. Playback still works.
