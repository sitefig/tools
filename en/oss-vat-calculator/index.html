OSS VAT Calculator (One-Stop-Shop VAT per Destination Country)

VAT per destination country and the total for an OSS return.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Under the One-Stop-Shop scheme you charge the VAT rate of the customer's country and report it in one return. Doing that by hand across countries is error-prone.

Paste country and amount lines. The tool uses standard rates from the bundled table, or a rate you give per line, and works from net or gross amounts. It does not file anything.

How to use it

  1. Paste lines of country code and amount, with an optional rate.
  2. Choose net or gross amounts and enter your own country.
  3. Read VAT per country and the total.

Frequently asked questions

Which rates are used?

Standard rates as of 13 July 2026 from the Your Europe page, unless you override them.

Why are my own country sales rejected?

Domestic sales go on your normal VAT return, not OSS.

Does it convert currency?

No. Enter amounts in the reporting currency.
