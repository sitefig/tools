Word Cloud Generator (From Text or Word Counts, SVG)

Counts words in your text, or reads word and count lines, and packs them into a cloud where size follows frequency.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Word clouds are blunt but quick for a look at a survey, a speech or a set of keywords. Paste raw text or your own counts; common English words are skipped for raw text so the interesting words stand out.

Layout is a spiral placement with rectangles, so words never overlap; those that do not fit are reported rather than shrunk silently. A table lists the counts, since size is a poor way to read exact values.

How to use it

  1. Paste text, or lines like word 12.
  2. Set the maximum words and tick rotation if you like.
  3. Change the seed for another layout.
  4. Copy or download the SVG.

Frequently asked questions

How does it know my input is counts?

If most lines end in a number it reads them as word and count.

Other languages?

Words are split on letters and digits of any script; the stop-word list is English only.

Why are some words missing?

They did not fit; lower the maximum or raise the size.

Is the layout stable?

Yes for the same input and seed.
