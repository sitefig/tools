Geography Quiz: Countries on a Blank World Map

A quiz on a blank world map: find the named country, or name the highlighted one. Choose a region and keep score.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Learn where countries are by testing yourself. In pointer mode the page names a country and you click it; after a miss it shows the right country. In keyboard mode a country is highlighted and you type its name, with suggestions, which works without a pointer.

Both modes score each country once and show your result at the end. The map has the countries of the 110m Natural Earth data, so very small states are not included. Region choices zoom the map to a continent.

How to use it

  1. Choose a region and a mode.
  2. Click the country named, or type the highlighted country's name.
  3. Press Next after a miss.
  4. Restart for a new shuffle.

Frequently asked questions

Why are some countries missing?

Small countries are not in the map data.

Can I use only a keyboard?

Yes, use the typing mode.

What counts as a right answer?

The name, an ISO code or a known alias.

Is progress stored?

No.
