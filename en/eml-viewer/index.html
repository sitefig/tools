EML Email Viewer (Headers, Body, Attachments)

Open a saved email message and see what is inside, including parts your mail client hides.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Paste the raw message source. The parser unfolds headers, decodes RFC 2047 encoded words in Subject and addresses, walks nested multipart structures, decodes base64 and quoted-printable bodies in their declared charsets and lists attachments with names, types and sizes. The HTML part is shown in a sandboxed frame with remote content blocked by a content-security policy, so tracking pixels do not load.

Output was compared with Python's email package. For Outlook .msg files use the Outlook MSG viewer.

No data is sent anywhere: the work happens in your browser, so you can use it offline once the page has loaded.

How to use it

  1. Open the .eml in a text editor and copy everything.
  2. Paste it in.
  3. Read the headers, text and structure.
  4. Use the header analyzer for the delivery path.

Frequently asked questions

Can it extract attachments?

It lists them. Downloading is not offered in this version.

Does viewing HTML load images?

No. External resources are blocked.

Why paste instead of choose a file?

Pasting works the same on every browser; the text is not uploaded.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything is processed in your browser, and the page makes no network requests with your input.
