YouTube Caption Converter (timedtext XML, json3, SRT, VTT to Text)

Convert YouTube caption formats into a clean transcript, SRT or VTT.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

YouTube serves captions as timedtext XML (srv1, srv3) or json3, and yt-dlp saves SRT or VTT. This converter reads all of them and writes SRT, WebVTT, a plain transcript or a transcript with [mm:ss] timestamps, merging the rolling repeated lines that auto-captions produce.

The page cannot download captions from YouTube; save them with your own tools (browser network tab, yt-dlp) and paste the data. Only transcribe or copy content you have the right to use.

How to use it

  1. Paste the caption data.
  2. Choose the output format.
  3. Copy the result.

Frequently asked questions

Can it fetch a video's transcript?

No. It converts text you provide; this site makes no requests.

What are rolling lines?

Auto-captions repeat the previous line while adding words; the merge option removes those repeats.

Which inputs work?

timedtext XML, json3, SRT and WebVTT.
