Running Pace Converter: Min per km, Min per Mile and Speed

Converts pace or speed between minutes per kilometre, minutes per mile, km/h and mph and predicts finish times at an even pace.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Races use kilometres in one country and miles in another. A pace of 5:30 per km is 8:51 per mile, 10.9 km/h.

Enter a pace like 5:30 or a speed and see all forms plus finish times for a mile, 5K, 10K, half marathon and marathon. The times assume the same pace throughout.

How to use it

  1. Enter a pace (5:30) or a speed.
  2. Choose the unit.
  3. Read the converted values and finish times.

Frequently asked questions

What is a 4 hour marathon pace?

About 5:41 per km or 9:09 per mile.

Does it adjust for hills?

No. It is straight arithmetic.

Which mile?

The international mile, 1.609344 km.
