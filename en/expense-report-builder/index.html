Expense Report Builder (VAT Split and CSV Export)

Turns pasted expense lines into totals by category and VAT rate, plus CSV.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Expense claims need net, VAT and gross per line and a summary by category. Paste CSV lines of date, category, description, amount and VAT percent.

Choose whether amounts include VAT. Whether VAT is deductible depends on national rules for meals, cars and similar items.

How to use it

  1. Paste one expense per line as CSV.
  2. Choose whether amounts include VAT.
  3. Copy the totals or the CSV.

Frequently asked questions

What delimiters work?

Comma, semicolon and tab, with quotes for text.

Is the data stored?

Nothing you type leaves your browser.

Is VAT reclaimable?

That depends on your country and the type of cost.
