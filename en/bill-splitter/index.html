Bill Splitter (Uneven Shares, Tax and Tip)

Fair split of a bill with individual items, a shared amount, tax and tip.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Equal splits are unfair when people order different amounts. List what each person ordered, enter the shared total, and give weights if some people share more.

Tax and tip are spread in proportion. Cents are allocated so that the shares add up to the exact total.

How to use it

  1. Write one line per person: name, items joined by +, weight.
  2. Enter the shared total, tax and tip.
  3. Read what each person pays.

Frequently asked questions

How are cents handled?

Fractions of a cent are assigned to the largest remainders so the sum matches.

Is tip before or after tax?

You choose.

What is weight?

The share of the shared items. 2 pays twice as much as 1.
