PDF N-Up Printer (Several Pages per Sheet)

Rearranges a PDF so that several pages share one sheet, scaled to fit, and saves the result as a new PDF ready to print.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Handouts, slide printouts and proof sheets use paper better with more than one page per side. Print dialogs can do this, but the result depends on the printer and on the program you use. Making the layout in the file gives you a PDF you can check and send anywhere.

Each original page is placed as a vector object, not a picture, so text stays sharp and searchable. Pages are scaled to their cell with margins and gaps you control, rotated pages are handled, and the orientation can follow the best fit automatically. Links, form fields and bookmarks are not carried over.

How to use it

  1. Choose a PDF.
  2. Pick pages per sheet, paper size and orientation.
  3. Set margin, gap and the border option.
  4. Press Run and download the new PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Does the quality drop?

No. Pages are placed as vector content and only scaled.

Which order are pages in?

Left to right, then down, by default. You can choose top to bottom, then right.

Can I mix page sizes?

Yes. Each page is scaled to fit its cell. The auto orientation looks at the first page.

Where do the links go?

They are dropped, together with annotations and form fields. Use this for print copies.
