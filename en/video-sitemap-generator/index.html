Video Sitemap Generator

Builds a video sitemap from simple key: value blocks and checks the required fields.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

A video sitemap lets search engines find videos on a page. Missing required fields or out-of-range values make an entry useless.

This tool checks the fields Google documents: thumbnail, title, description of up to 2048 characters, a content or player URL, duration from 1 to 28800 seconds, rating 0 to 5 and at most 32 tags. It stops with a list of problems instead of writing a broken file.

How to use it

  1. Write one block per video, separated by a blank line.
  2. Fill page, thumbnail, title, description and content or player.
  3. Add optional fields such as duration or published.
  4. Copy the XML into your sitemap file.

Frequently asked questions

Content or player URL?

At least one is required. Content is recommended when you have it.

Which date format?

W3C: 2026-05-01 or with time and zone.

Are restriction and platform supported?

No. Add them by hand if needed.

Can one page have several videos?

Yes. Repeat the same page value.
