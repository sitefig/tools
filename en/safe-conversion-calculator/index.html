SAFE Conversion Calculator (Valuation Cap and Discount)

Estimates conversion of a single SAFE using a valuation cap and a discount at a priced round.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

A SAFE converts at the lower of the cap price and the discounted round price. For a post-money SAFE, the cap price equals the cap less the SAFE amount divided by existing shares, so an uncapped ownership equals amount divided by cap.

The model covers one SAFE and does not count other SAFEs, notes or a pool increase. Real conversions follow the signed document and a lawyer's cap table. Treat the result as a sanity check.

How to use it

  1. Choose pre-money or post-money SAFE.
  2. Enter amount, cap, discount, existing shares and the round price per share.
  3. Read the conversion price, shares and ownership.

Frequently asked questions

Which price applies?

The lower of the cap price and the discount price.

Can I model several SAFEs?

Not in this tool. They interact in post-money SAFEs.

Is this exact?

No. It follows public descriptions and your document may differ.
