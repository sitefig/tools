SoftwareApplication Schema Generator

Generates app markup with Google's application categories, operating system, price (0 for free apps) and an aggregate rating or review.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Google requires a name, a price and either a rating or a review for software app results. The tool checks the category against the list in Google's documentation, writes free apps as price 0 with a currency, and warns when no rating or review is present.

Pick SoftwareApplication, MobileApplication or WebApplication as the type. Ratings must be genuine and shown on the page.

How to use it

  1. Choose the type.
  2. Fill name, operatingSystem and category.
  3. Enter price and currency; use 0 for a free app.
  4. Add ratingValue and ratingCount, then copy the script.

Frequently asked questions

Which categories are valid?

The ones Google lists, from GameApplication to ReferenceApplication. Others trigger a warning.

How is a free app written?

As an offer with price 0 and a currency.

Do I need both rating and review?

One of them is enough for Google.

Which operating systems can I list?

Free text such as Android 10+, iOS 16+.
