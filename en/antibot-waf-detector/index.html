Anti-bot and WAF Detector (from Pasted Headers, Cookies and HTML)

Find out which bot protection, WAF or CAPTCHA produced a response, from public signatures in its headers, cookies and HTML.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

When a request returns 403, a challenge page or odd cookies, the first question is which product is in front of the site. This tool matches the pasted text against public markers: server and diagnostic headers, characteristic cookie names, challenge script paths and widget markup for Cloudflare (including Turnstile), Akamai, DataDome, HUMAN/PerimeterX, Imperva, Kasada, F5, AWS WAF, Sucuri, Fastly, Vercel, reCAPTCHA, hCaptcha, Arkose and others.

A match shows a product is deployed, not how it is configured, and absence of a match proves nothing: responses vary by visitor and the paste may be incomplete. Use it to audit your own sites, understand blocked monitoring or write accurate support tickets.

How to use it

  1. Copy the response headers (curl -i) and, if possible, the first part of the HTML.
  2. Paste everything, including Set-Cookie lines.
  3. Read the detected products and the evidence for each.

Frequently asked questions

Does it bypass protection?

No. It only names what is visible in text you already have.

Why nothing detected?

The response may be from a site without such protection, or the paste lacks the cookies and scripts that carry the markers.

Is it accurate?

Signatures are public cookie, header and script names. Rebranded or customised deployments can hide them.
