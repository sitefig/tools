Noise Generator

Play steady white, pink or brown noise with a volume control. The sound is generated live in your browser and loops until you press stop.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Steady background noise helps some people concentrate in an open office, settle a baby, mask a noisy neighbour or fall asleep. Streaming sites and apps can do this, but they add adverts, require sign-in or stop when the connection drops.

This generator creates the sound itself, with no audio files to download. White noise has equal energy at every frequency and sounds like a hiss; pink noise falls off toward the highs and sounds softer, like steady rain; brown noise is deeper still, closer to a low rumble. A four-second buffer is built when you press start and looped, so playback is gap-free. Keep the volume low, since long listening at high levels can harm your hearing.

How to use it

  1. Choose a type: white, pink or brown.
  2. Set the volume with the slider before you start.
  3. Press Start to begin playback; press it again to stop.
  4. Switch type at any time and the sound restarts with the new colour.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between the noise types?

White noise is bright and hissy. Pink noise reduces high frequencies and sounds more natural. Brown noise emphasises low frequencies and sounds like a deep rumble. Try each and keep the one that feels comfortable.

Will it keep playing in the background?

It plays while the page is open. Some phones pause web audio when the screen locks or the tab is closed, so test on your device before relying on it overnight.

Is there a timer?

No. It plays until you stop it or close the page. If you want it to end automatically, use a separate countdown timer alongside it.

Is anything downloaded or recorded?

No audio files are fetched and nothing is recorded or uploaded. The samples are computed in your browser from random numbers using the Web Audio API.
