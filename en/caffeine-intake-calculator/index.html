Caffeine Intake Calculator (Daily Total vs 400 mg)

Add up your caffeine and compare it with 400 mg a day.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Type your drinks one per line, such as coffee 2 or energy drink 1, or give an exact amount like mg 80 2. The page shows a low and high total using the FDA ranges per 12 fl oz serving and compares it with the 400 mg a day figure the FDA gives for most healthy adults.

A small espresso, a big mug or a branded energy drink will differ from these averages, so read the can. Sensitivity varies a lot. If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, have a heart condition or take medication, ask a health professional for your own limit.

How to use it

  1. Type one drink and count per line.
  2. Use mg lines for labelled products.
  3. Read the low and high total.
  4. Compare it with the 400 mg figure.

Frequently asked questions

Where do the amounts come from?

From the FDA page on caffeine for a 12 fl oz serving: coffee 113 to 247 mg, black tea 71, green tea 37, cola 23 to 83, energy drinks 41 to 246.

Is 400 mg a limit for everyone?

No. It is a figure for most healthy adults. Children, pregnancy and some conditions call for less.

Is this medical advice?

No. It is an estimate from a published formula. A doctor or dietitian can judge your own case far better.

Is anything uploaded?

No. The work happens in your browser and the page makes no network request with your input.
