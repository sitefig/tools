Payment Fee Gross-Up Calculator (Charge So You Net a Target Amount)

Calculates a processing fee on a charge, or the charge you need so that the amount you receive after fees is a target.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

If you want to receive 100 and the processor takes 2.9 percent plus 0.30, charging 100 leaves you short. The right charge is (100 + 0.30) divided by (1 - 0.029). People often add 2.9 percent to 100, which is slightly too little.

Enter the percentage and fixed fee from your processor's pricing page. The tool shows the fee, the charge and a check that works the fee back to your target. Passing fees on to customers is restricted in some places, so check the rules where you sell.

How to use it

  1. Choose whether you want the fee on a charge or the charge for a net.
  2. Enter the amount and your percentage and fixed fee.
  3. Read the result and the check line.

Frequently asked questions

Which fees should I enter?

The ones in your own processor contract or pricing page. Rates vary by country, card type and volume.

Is surcharging allowed?

Not everywhere, and card networks have rules. Check before you pass fees on.

Does it handle currency conversion?

No. Add a conversion fee to the percentage if it applies.
