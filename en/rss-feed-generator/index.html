RSS Feed Generator

Write your posts as simple lines and get a complete RSS 2.0 file to host on your site.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Each line has the form "title | URL | YYYY-MM-DD | summary". The generator validates the URLs, converts the dates to RFC 822 form, uses each URL as a permalink GUID, escapes special characters and adds the channel details you fill in (title, site URL, description, language, feed URL). The result can be saved as feed.xml.

For a site with many posts it is better to generate the feed from your CMS or static-site generator; this tool is for small sites, tests and examples. Check the output with the RSS validator.

No data is sent anywhere: the work happens in your browser, so you can use it offline once the page has loaded.

How to use it

  1. Fill in the feed title, site URL, description and feed URL.
  2. Add one line per post.
  3. Copy the XML into feed.xml on your site.
  4. Link it with <link rel="alternate" type="application/rss+xml" href="/feed.xml">.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a summary?

No, but readers show it. Without a description the item needs a title.

What if a title contains &?

It is escaped automatically.

Atom or RSS?

Both are widely supported. RSS 2.0 is simpler and what this tool writes.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything is processed in your browser, and the page makes no network requests with your input.
