US Privacy String (CCPA usprivacy) Decoder

Explains each character of a legacy CCPA US Privacy string.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

The US Privacy string is four characters, such as 1YNN: version, whether notice and opt-out choice were given, whether the user opted out of sale, and whether the transaction is covered by the IAB Limited Service Provider Agreement.

This tool spells out each position. IAB Tech Lab states that the signal was deprecated on 31 January 2024 in favour of the Global Privacy Platform, but you still meet the string in old tags, cookies and logs.

How to use it

  1. Copy the usprivacy value from a cookie or ad request.
  2. Paste it into the box.
  3. Read what each position says.
  4. Use the GPP decoder if your site now uses GPP.

Frequently asked questions

What does a dash mean?

Unknown or not applicable. 1--- means the CCPA does not apply.

Is the string still used?

It was deprecated in January 2024 but still appears in older setups.

Can a dash appear anywhere?

In position 3 only when the CCPA does not apply.
