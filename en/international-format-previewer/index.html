International Date, Number and Currency Format Previewer

Shows how one value is written in the locales you choose, side by side.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

1,234.56 in the US is 1.234,56 in Germany and 1 234,56 in France. Dates change order and separators, and some locales use other digits. Pick locales, enter a number, a currency and a date, and compare.

The output comes from your browser's Intl implementation with CLDR data, which is what your own site would use. The table is plain text so you can copy it into a brief for translators.

How to use it

  1. List the locales.
  2. Set the number, currency and date.
  3. Read the table.
  4. Copy it into your localisation notes.

Frequently asked questions

Why do browsers differ?

They ship different CLDR versions.

Is the date in UTC?

Yes, so output is the same wherever you are.

Can I add other fields?

Not here; this covers numbers, currency, percent and dates.

Why do some locales show other digits?

Locales like ar-EG use their own numbering system by default.
