Video Trimmer: Cut a Clip by Start and End Time

Cuts a video file to the start and end times you choose and saves just that section. It runs in your browser and the file never leaves your device.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Most online trimmers upload your video to a server. This one plays your file in a hidden video element, copies the chosen section to a canvas and records it with the browser recorder. Your footage stays on your device, which matters for private or large files.

It records in real time, so a 60 second clip takes about a minute and the tab must stay visible. The result is re-encoded, not a lossless cut, so it is best for sharing clips, not for archiving masters. Use the player and the "Use player time" buttons to set the points by eye.

How to use it

  1. Choose a video file and watch it in the preview player.
  2. Pause on the first frame you want and press Use player time next to Start. Do the same for End.
  3. Press Process and keep the tab open until it finishes.
  4. Play the result and download it.

Frequently asked questions

Is the cut lossless?

No. The section is decoded and recorded again, so there is a small quality loss. A lossless cut needs a tool that copies the stream, which browsers cannot do for most files.

Why does it take as long as the clip?

The browser records the video as it plays. There is no faster path in the platform APIs.

Which formats can I open?

Whatever your browser can play, usually MP4 (H.264), WebM and often MOV. The output is WebM, or MP4 where your browser supports it.

Is my video uploaded?

No. Everything happens in this tab.
