Disposable Email Detector (Throwaway Domains)

Paste addresses to see which use a known throwaway email provider.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Disposable addresses sign up for a free offer, then vanish. They inflate list size, bounce later and skew your numbers. Flagging them before import lets you decide what to do.

The list is short, written from general knowledge, and not checked against a published list. New throwaway domains appear every day, so "ok" never means the address is real. For stronger checks you need a maintained list and a server.

How to use it

  1. Paste the addresses.
  2. Read which are marked DISPOSABLE.
  3. Remove or review those entries.
  4. Use a confirmation email for the rest.

Frequently asked questions

How many domains are in the list?

About 75 well-known ones. It is not complete.

Can it catch subdomains?

Yes, a subdomain of a listed domain is flagged.

Does ok mean valid?

No.

Is the list uploaded?

No.
