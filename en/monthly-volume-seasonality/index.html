Search Volume Seasonality Chart from Monthly Data

Turns monthly search volume figures into a seasonality chart: average per calendar month, index against the average, peak, low and swing. Several years are averaged.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Many topics peak in one season. Knowing the months helps you plan when to publish and refresh. Keyword tools give monthly volumes, but not a quick picture.

The tool averages each calendar month across years and shows an index where 100 is the average month. With less than two years, one-off events can look like seasons, and monthly volumes from keyword tools are estimates. For Google Trends data use the Google Trends CSV Analyzer.

How to use it

  1. Paste the monthly volumes in any of the supported formats.
  2. If you paste bare numbers, choose the first month.
  3. Read the chart and the peak and low months.
  4. Plan to publish or refresh a few weeks before the rise.

Frequently asked questions

Which formats can I paste?

2024-01 1200, Jan 2024: 1,200, Jan 1200, or one number per line from your chosen first month.

How much data do I need?

At least six different months, ideally 24 or more.

What does the index mean?

Volume divided by the average month, times 100. 150 means 50% above average.

Is the publish timing advice exact?

No. It is a rule of thumb. Test it on your own pages.
