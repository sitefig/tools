VAT Calculator

Enter an amount and a VAT rate, then choose whether the amount is net (add VAT) or gross (remove VAT). The result shows net, VAT and gross.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Invoices, price lists and shop tags mix prices with and without VAT, and the wrong direction of calculation is a common slip. Taking 21% off a gross price is not the same as dividing by 1.21, and the first approach leaves you with too much. Freelancers, shopkeepers and bookkeepers meet this every time they raise an invoice or reconcile a receipt. This calculator handles both directions with whatever rate you type, so it suits any country and any reduced rate. It shows the formula and the three figures side by side. It is a calculation aid only and does not check VAT numbers or tell you which rate applies. Everything stays in your browser.

How to use it

  1. Enter the amount and the VAT rate in percent.
  2. Choose 'Add VAT (amount is net)' or 'Remove VAT (amount is gross)'.
  3. Read the net, VAT and gross amounts and copy them if needed.

Frequently asked questions

How do I remove VAT from a gross price?

Divide the gross price by 1 plus the rate. At 21%, divide by 1.21. Subtracting 21% of the gross price is wrong because VAT is calculated on the net price.

Which VAT rate should I use?

The one that applies to your goods or services in your country. The tool does not look rates up, so check with your tax authority. Many places have a standard rate and reduced rates.

Does it round to two decimals?

Results are displayed to two decimals. Invoices may round at line level or total level depending on local rules, so small differences from your accounting system are possible.

Is this tax advice?

No. It is an arithmetic helper. Your obligations depend on your jurisdiction and circumstances, so ask an accountant when in doubt.
