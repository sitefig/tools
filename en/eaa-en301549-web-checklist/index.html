EAA and EN 301 549 Web Content Checklist (Clause 9 Status Summary)

Summarises your status for each EN 301 549 clause 9 web requirement.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

The European Accessibility Act points to EN 301 549, whose web clause mirrors WCAG 2.1 A and AA.

Paste a status per clause and get counts, a table by principle and the open items. You can also print a blank checklist. It covers web content only, not documents, software or functional performance. The mapping was derived from the WCAG text and prior knowledge of EN 301 549 V3.2.1; the standard was not available to check.

How to use it

  1. Choose the blank checklist to copy it.
  2. Add met, partial, not or na after each clause.
  3. Switch to the summary and paste your lines.
  4. Work through the open items.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a compliance assessment?

No. It records your own judgement.

Does the EAA apply to me?

That depends on your products and services; ask a legal adviser.

Why not WCAG 2.2?

EN 301 549 V3.2.1 refers to WCAG 2.1.

Is it uploaded?

No.
