Flow Rate Converter: Litres per Minute, Gallons per Hour, m3/s

Converts flow rates between litres per minute or second, cubic metres per hour or second, US and UK gallons per minute or hour and cubic feet per minute.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Pump, shower and ventilation specs use different flow units: L/min, GPM, m3/h or CFM. Comparing them needs the right gallon.

This tool shows all units at once, with US and UK gallons separate.

How to use it

  1. Enter the flow rate.
  2. Choose its unit.
  3. Read the other units.

Frequently asked questions

How many L/min is 1 GPM?

1 US gallon per minute is 3.785 L/min.

What is CFM?

Cubic feet per minute, used for fans and air flow.

Does it handle mass flow?

No, only volume flow.
