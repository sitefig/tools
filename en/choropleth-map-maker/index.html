Choropleth World Map Maker (Colour Countries from a CSV)

Shades a world map by value for each country you list, with classes, a legend and a table of matches.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Paste country and value rows and get a map. Names, ISO 2-letter and ISO 3-letter codes are matched, with a list of common alternative names. Anything that cannot be matched is listed so you can fix the spelling.

The shapes are Natural Earth 1:110m, public domain, stored on the site; small countries and islands are missing at this scale, so they cannot be coloured. Choose equal-interval or quantile classes and the colour scale. Colour alone is not enough, so the table has every value.

How to use it

  1. Paste rows: country, value.
  2. Choose the colour scale, the number of classes and the break method.
  3. Check the unmatched list in the note.
  4. Copy or download the SVG.

Frequently asked questions

Which names work?

English short names, ISO codes and many variants such as USA, UK or Ivory Coast.

Why is a small country missing?

The 110m data omits it.

Quantile or equal interval?

Quantile gives each colour the same number of countries; equal interval keeps value gaps even.

Is the map projection accurate?

It is plate carree (plain latitude and longitude), which stretches areas near the poles.
