CAC and LTV:CAC Ratio Calculator (with Payback)

Enter spend, new customers, revenue, margin and lifespan to get CAC, LTV, the ratio and payback.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

CAC alone says little. Set against what a customer returns, it shows whether growth pays for itself, and how many months it takes to earn the acquisition cost back.

LTV here is gross profit over the lifespan, or over 1 divided by monthly churn if you enter churn. The 3:1 ratio is a common rule of thumb, not a law. Count salaries and tools in the spend for an honest CAC.

How to use it

  1. Enter marketing and sales spend and new customers for a period.
  2. Enter revenue per customer per month and margin.
  3. Enter lifespan or churn.
  4. Read CAC, LTV, ratio and payback.

Frequently asked questions

Is 3:1 a rule?

It is a common benchmark. Context such as cash and growth stage matters.

Which period?

Use the same period for spend and new customers.

Revenue or profit?

The tool uses gross profit via margin.

Is data stored?

No.
