Clothing Size Converter (Women US, UK, DE, FR, IT and Men Letters to EU)

Looks up equivalent clothing sizes between common systems from a published reference table.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Clothing sizes are not defined by one standard, and vanity sizing makes labels drift. A table gives a fair starting point, not a promise of fit.

The women's numbers and men's letters come from the Wikipedia article on clothing sizes. For women, enter the number in the system you know. For men, enter a letter, an EU number or a chest size in cm.

How to use it

  1. Choose women or men.
  2. For women pick your system and enter the size; for men enter a letter, EU number or chest cm.
  3. Read the equivalents and then check the brand's own chart.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my size differ by brand?

Brands use different cuts and vanity sizing. Use the brand's measurements.

Where does the table come from?

From the Wikipedia article on clothing sizes, as noted in the output.

Are children's sizes included?

No.
