Rule of 40 Calculator for SaaS Growth and Profit

Adds growth rate and a profit margin to check the Rule of 40.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

The Rule of 40 says growth rate plus profit margin should be at least 40 percent for a healthy software company. It lets a company trade growth against profit.

Choose one margin definition, such as EBITDA or free cash flow, and keep it the same over time. The rule is a rough guide, mostly used for scaled companies.

How to use it

  1. Enter yearly revenue growth.
  2. Enter the margin and pick its type.
  3. Read the score.

Frequently asked questions

Which margin should I use?

The one your investors use; just stay consistent.

Does it apply to early startups?

Less so. Early on, growth matters more.

Is it a law?

No, it is a rule of thumb.
