Live Meeting Cost Timer

A timer that converts attendee time into money as the meeting goes on.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Meetings feel free because nobody sees the bill. Showing the running cost in money makes a long agenda and a late start concrete.

The cost uses salary over working hours only, so it understates the real cost because employer charges are not added. The total is announced when you pause, while the running amount updates visually. Nothing is stored or sent.

How to use it

  1. Enter attendees, average salary and working hours.
  2. Press Start when the meeting begins.
  3. Press Pause to read the total.

Frequently asked questions

Is the cost accurate?

It is an estimate: salary time only, without employer charges or lost focus.

Can I use hourly pay?

Enter hourly pay times working hours as a yearly figure.

Is anything saved?

No.
