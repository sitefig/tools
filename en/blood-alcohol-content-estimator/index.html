Blood Alcohol Content Estimator (Widmark Formula)

Estimate blood alcohol from your drinks, weight and time.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

List your drinks as count, millilitres and percent alcohol, then enter your weight, sex and the hours since your first drink. The page uses the Widmark formula, BAC = A / (r x W) - beta x t, with alcohol at 0.789 g per ml, r of 0.68 for men and 0.55 for women, and an elimination rate you can change (default 0.015 g/dL per hour).

It is a rough guide only. Food, medication, drinking pace and body differences move the real figure a lot. It assumes all the alcohol is absorbed at once, so it overstates the peak for slow drinking. It is not a test of whether you can drive. Do not drive after drinking.

How to use it

  1. Type one drink per line, for example 2 x 330 5.
  2. Choose sex and enter weight and hours.
  3. Adjust the elimination rate if you wish.
  4. Read the estimate and the time to zero.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use this to decide if I can drive?

No. Never use it for that. The estimate can be wrong in both directions, and legal limits differ by country.

Why do women get a lower r?

Widmark factors reflect average body water share, which differs between men and women on average. Individuals vary a lot.

What does per mille mean?

Grams of alcohol per litre of blood. 0.50 per mille equals 0.05 g/dL.

Is anything uploaded?

No. The work happens in your browser and the page makes no network request with your input.
