Line Break Remover (Unwrap Hard-Wrapped Text)

Join text copied from a PDF or email back into flowing paragraphs, keeping real paragraph breaks, bullet lists and optionally mending hyphenated words.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Text copied from a PDF or a plain-text email breaks at every line end. Pasting it into a document gives a ragged column of short lines.

This tool joins the lines of each paragraph, keeps blank-line paragraph breaks, can keep bullets and numbered items on their own lines, and joins words split with a hyphen at the line end. Or remove every break. A word such as well-known that happens to break at its hyphen is also joined, so check the result.

How to use it

  1. Paste the wrapped text.
  2. Choose whether to mend hyphenated line ends and keep list items.
  3. Tick the last option to get one single line.
  4. Copy the result.

Frequently asked questions

Why are some hyphens removed?

A hyphen at a line end followed by a lowercase letter is treated as a split word. A real compound such as well-known may lose its hyphen, so read the result.

How are paragraphs found?

A blank line starts a new paragraph. Text with no blank lines becomes one paragraph.

Does it change my words?

No. Only line breaks, spaces and split hyphens change.
