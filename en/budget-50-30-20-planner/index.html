Budget Planner (50/30/20 Rule) with Actual Spending Check

Target amounts for needs, wants and savings, checked against the spending you paste in.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

The 50/30/20 rule is a simple starting point: half for needs, 30% for wants, 20% for savings and debt repayment. The shares are editable here, because high-rent cities often push needs above half.

Paste your spending as category, label and amount lines to see which category is over or under. The sheet is not stored.

How to use it

  1. Enter monthly take-home pay.
  2. Adjust the percentages if needed.
  3. Paste spending lines with need, want or save.
  4. Read the difference per category.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as a need?

Costs you must pay: housing, food, utilities, insurance, minimum debt payments, transport to work.

Do debt repayments go in savings?

The rule groups extra debt repayment with savings. Minimum payments count as needs.

Is data saved?

Nothing you type leaves your browser.
