Heroku Router Log Parser (Dyno, Service Time, H-Code Errors)

Parses Heroku router lines and counts H-code errors with their official titles.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Router lines show what the platform saw: connect time, service time, status and, for failures, an H-code. H12 request timeout is the one most people look for.

H-code titles come from the Heroku error codes page. A service time near 30000 ms with a 503 is the pattern of a timeout.

How to use it

  1. Run heroku logs --source heroku --dyno router -n 1500.
  2. Paste the output.
  3. Filter by status if needed.
  4. Read H-codes, latency and slow paths.

Frequently asked questions

Which H-codes are known?

The codes listed on the Heroku error codes page, from H10 to H99. H16 and H23 are no longer used.

What are the times?

connect and service are in milliseconds, as printed by the router.

Does it read app logs?

No, only heroku[router] lines. Others are counted as skipped.

Is my log uploaded?

No. Everything is processed in your browser and nothing is sent. Logs can hold IP addresses and tokens, so the page does not write them into the address bar.
