MRR and ARR Calculator With Net New MRR

Calculates monthly and annual recurring revenue and the monthly movement of MRR.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

MRR is the sum of recurring monthly revenue and ARR is twelve times that. The useful view is the movement: new plus expansion minus contraction minus churn gives net new MRR and a growth rate.

List plans as name, price, subscribers, then enter the movement components. Define MRR the way your investors do, usually excluding one-off fees and uncertain usage.

How to use it

  1. List plans with price and subscribers.
  2. Enter new, expansion, contraction and churned MRR.
  3. Read MRR, ARR and growth.

Frequently asked questions

What belongs in MRR?

Recurring subscription revenue only, normalised to a month.

How do annual plans count?

Divide by 12.

Is ARR just MRR x 12?

Yes, as a run rate, not a forecast.
