Value Proposition Statement Generator (Canvas to Sentence)

Fill in the canvas fields and get a fill-in sentence, a positioning statement and headline options.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Many value propositions are vague. Forcing the pieces into a pattern - who it is for, the job, the pain, how you relieve it - shows which piece you cannot yet say clearly.

The patterns are the common fill-in and positioning templates. The output is a draft to edit, not finished copy. Check that it names a real customer and could not be claimed by every competitor.

How to use it

  1. Describe the customer and their job, pain and gain.
  2. Add your product, category and how it relieves the pain.
  3. Read the three outputs.
  4. Edit and test the wording with real customers.

Frequently asked questions
