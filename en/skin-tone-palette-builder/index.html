Skin Tone Palette Builder for Illustration

Builds a spread of skin tones or takes your base tones and adds a shadow and a highlight for each.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Illustrators need a believable set of skin colours with matching shading. In OKLCH, shadows get slightly redder and more saturated and highlights lighter and softer, a common painting habit. You control the strength of each.

Tones are generated by a simple rule, not measured from people, and the tool makes no claim about categories. Check them against your artwork and with the people you depict. To sample from a photo, use the Color Picker from Image and paste the colours here.

How to use it

  1. Leave the box empty to generate a range, or paste base colours.
  2. Set the shadow and highlight strength.
  3. Run the tool.
  4. Copy the colours.

Frequently asked questions

Does it include a photo sampler?

Use the Color Picker from Image for that, then paste the colours here.

Are the presets standard skin tone scales?

No. They are generated by a rule and are not the Fitzpatrick or any other published scale.

Can I use them for emoji?

They are only colours. Real emoji skin tones are defined by platform artwork.

What is the strength setting?

Zero gives almost no shift, 100 a strong one.
