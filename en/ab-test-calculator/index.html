A/B Test Significance Calculator

Enter visitors and conversions for two variants and see the conversion rates, z-score, p-value, whether the difference is significant, and the sample size needed to detect a given lift.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Variant B converts at 5 percent, A at 4 percent, so B wins? Not necessarily. With few visitors a gap like that is often noise, and stopping a test the moment it looks good is a common way to ship changes that do nothing.

This calculator runs a pooled two-proportion z-test and reports the p-value, a confidence interval for the difference, and a clear yes or no at 90, 95 or 99 percent confidence. It also estimates the number of visitors per variant you need to detect a relative lift of your choice, so you can plan the test before you start. It is a standard fixed-sample test, run in your browser.

How to use it

  1. Enter visitors and conversions for variant A and variant B.
  2. Choose the confidence level: 90, 95 or 99 percent.
  3. Set the relative lift you want to detect and the power for the sample size estimate.
  4. Read the p-value, the significance verdict, the confidence interval and the required sample size.

Frequently asked questions

What does the p-value mean?

It is the probability of seeing a difference at least this large if the variants really performed the same. A p-value below 0.05 is significant at 95 percent confidence. It does not give the probability that B is better.

Why shouldn't I stop the test as soon as it is significant?

Checking repeatedly and stopping at the first significant result inflates false positives. This is a fixed-sample test. Decide the sample size in advance, then evaluate once it is reached.

How is the sample size estimated?

From the baseline rate of A, the relative lift you want to detect, your confidence and the power, using the standard two-proportion formula. It gives visitors per variant and in total.

Does it handle more than two variants?

No, only two. For several variants you need to correct for multiple comparisons, which this tool does not do.
