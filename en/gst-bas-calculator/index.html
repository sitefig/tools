GST and BAS Worksheet Calculator (Australia, 10%)

Total your sales and purchases and see the GST figures that go on a BAS.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Enter one line per transaction as sale or purchase, the amount and whether it is GST inclusive, exclusive or GST-free. The calculator splits each amount, then totals the main BAS labels: G1 total sales, 1A GST on sales, G11 non-capital purchases, 1B GST on purchases and the net GST payable or refundable. The rate is editable.

It is a worksheet to understand the figures. Real BAS classification involves capital purchases (G10), private use, input-taxed supplies and adjustments, so lodge through the ATO portal or your agent. This is not tax advice.

How to use it

  1. List each transaction as sale|purchase, amount, inc|ex|free.
  2. Check the rate (10% in Australia).
  3. Read the BAS figures and the net GST.

Frequently asked questions

What is 1A and 1B?

1A is GST collected on sales; 1B is GST credits on purchases.

How is GST-inclusive handled?

GST is one eleventh of a GST-inclusive amount at 10%.

Does it lodge anything?

No, it only calculates.
