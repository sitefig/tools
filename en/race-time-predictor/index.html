Race Time Predictor (Riegel Formula for 5K to Marathon)

Predict your 5K, 10K, half marathon and marathon times.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Enter a recent race distance and finish time. The page applies the Riegel formula, T2 = T1 x (D2 / D1) raised to 1.06, and lists predicted times and paces for the other distances.

The formula assumes you have trained for the longer race. For a marathon predicted from a 5K, treat the result as an upper limit of what you could do with solid mileage. You can change the exponent if you know a better one for yourself.

How to use it

  1. Choose the unit.
  2. Enter your recent race distance and time.
  3. Keep 1.06 or set your own exponent.
  4. Read the predicted times and paces.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is Riegel?

It works best between about 5K and the half marathon for trained runners. Errors grow towards the marathon.

Why is my marathon prediction too fast?

Short races do not show how well you handle fatigue over 3 hours. Low weekly mileage is the usual reason.

Can I use miles?

Yes. Switch the unit and enter the distance in miles.

Is anything uploaded?

No. The work happens in your browser and the page makes no network request with your input.
