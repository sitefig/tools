Spam Trigger Word Checker (Subject and Body)

Paste a subject and body to see words and phrases often cited as spam triggers highlighted in the text, with a count of matches.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Long lists of spam words circulate, and checking an email against them by eye is tedious. This page marks the matches for you so you can decide which to rewrite, and counts them in the subject and the body separately.

Be realistic about what this tells you. Modern filters score sender reputation, authentication, links and engagement far more than single words, and plenty of legitimate email says "free". Treat every match as a prompt to read the sentence again, not as a failure. Your text stays in the browser.

How to use it

  1. Paste the subject and the body.
  2. Read the highlighted matches and the list under them.
  3. Rewrite phrases that sound like a pitch.
  4. Run it again to confirm the count went down.

Frequently asked questions

Will an email with no matches reach the inbox?

Not necessarily. Authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), list quality and complaints matter more.

Is this the list a filter uses?

No. It is a hand-made list of commonly cited words. No filter publishes its rules.

Does case matter?

No, matching ignores capitals.

Is my email sent anywhere?

No. It is checked in your browser.
