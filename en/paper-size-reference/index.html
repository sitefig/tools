Paper Size Reference: A, B, C and US Sizes in mm, Inches, Points and Pixels

Shows ISO A, B and C sizes, DL and US sizes such as Letter, Legal and Tabloid in millimetres, inches, points and pixels at a chosen DPI.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Printing, PDF and design work need the exact page size. A4 is 210 x 297 mm and US Letter is 8.5 x 11 inches, which are not the same shape.

Pick a size to see it in every unit, including pixels at 72, 150 or 300 DPI. The ISO sizes follow the published millimetre values; the ISO text itself was not checked.

How to use it

  1. Choose a paper size or All sizes.
  2. Set the DPI.
  3. Read the sizes in mm, inches, points and pixels.

Frequently asked questions

What is A4 in pixels?

2480 x 3508 px at 300 DPI, 595 x 842 at 72 DPI.

Why is Letter different from A4?

They come from different standards. Letter is a little wider and shorter.

Does it show landscape?

Sizes are portrait, width first. Swap them for landscape.
