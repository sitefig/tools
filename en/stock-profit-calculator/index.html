Stock Profit Calculator (Gain or Loss After Fees)

Profit, return and break-even price for a trade including broker fees.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Commissions matter more on small trades. The tool adds the buy fee to your cost and subtracts the sell fee from proceeds, then shows the break-even sell price.

An optional flat tax rate on gains is available. Real tax rules (allowances, loss offsets) are country specific and not modelled.

How to use it

  1. Enter shares, buy price and sell price.
  2. Enter commissions on both sides.
  3. Read profit, percentage and break-even.

Frequently asked questions

Is the break-even price exact?

Yes, for the fees you entered.

Does it handle several lots?

No. See the crypto and shares capital gains calculator for FIFO.

Does it handle currency?

No. Use one currency.
