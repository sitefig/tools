Rent vs Buy Calculator (Wealth After N Years)

Estimates which path leaves you with more wealth after a number of years.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

The answer depends on assumptions you choose: price growth, rent growth, investment return and costs. The tool models both sides month by month and lets whoever pays less invest the difference.

It leaves out income tax on returns, mortgage interest relief and changing rates. Change a few inputs to see how sensitive the result is before deciding anything.

How to use it

  1. Enter price, down payment, rate and term.
  2. Enter upkeep, growth, rent and investment return.
  3. Choose the number of years and read the result.

Frequently asked questions

Does it include tax?

No tax effects are modelled.

Why is upkeep a percent of value?

It combines maintenance and property tax, which usually scale with value.

Is it advice?

No. It is a model of your assumptions.
