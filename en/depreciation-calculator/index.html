Depreciation Calculator (Straight-Line, Declining Balance, Sum-of-Years)

Yearly depreciation, accumulated depreciation and book value.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Depreciation spreads the cost of an asset over its life. Straight-line is even, declining balance front-loads, and sum-of-years-digits front-loads less sharply.

The schedule never goes below residual value and uses whole years. Tax rules may require different methods or rates.

How to use it

  1. Enter cost, residual value and life.
  2. Choose the method.
  3. Read the table.

Frequently asked questions

What is the factor?

Declining balance uses factor divided by life as the yearly rate. 2 means double declining.

Does it handle part years?

No.

Does it match tax rules?

Not necessarily.
