Sunrise, Sunset and Day Length Calculator for Any Latitude and Date

Calculates sunrise, solar noon, sunset and day length for coordinates and a date with the NOAA solar equations, shown in a time zone you choose.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Many sunrise sites call a weather API. This one calculates it on your device from latitude, longitude and date, so you can use it anywhere.

It matches the astral library within about 1 minute on nine test locations. Real times vary with weather, altitude and the horizon. Use east-positive longitude.

How to use it

  1. Enter latitude and longitude.
  2. Pick a date and the time zone to show.
  3. Read sunrise, sunset and day length.

Frequently asked questions

Can it find a place by name?

No, that needs a lookup service. Enter coordinates from a map.

How accurate is it?

About a minute against astral at mid-latitudes; less reliable near the poles.

What about polar day?

It tells you when the sun stays up or down all day.
