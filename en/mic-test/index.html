Microphone Test (Level Meter, Record and Playback)

Find out whether your mic works and how loud it is before a call or recording.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

After you allow access, the page shows the available inputs, a live meter in dBFS with the loudest level so far and a plain-language hint (too quiet, near clipping, fine). Recording uses the browser's MediaRecorder for five seconds and plays the clip back in a player; nothing is sent over the network.

Use headphones for playback to avoid feedback. Browsers only show device names after you grant permission.

No data is sent anywhere: the work happens in your browser, so you can use it offline once the page has loaded.

How to use it

  1. Press Start and allow the microphone.
  2. Speak and watch the meter.
  3. Record five seconds and play it back.
  4. Choose another input if the level is zero.

Frequently asked questions

Is my voice uploaded?

No. Audio is processed in the page.

Why is the level very low?

Wrong input selected, muted device, or input gain set too low in the system settings.

What is dBFS?

Decibels relative to full scale; 0 is the maximum before clipping.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything is processed in your browser, and the page makes no network requests with your input.
