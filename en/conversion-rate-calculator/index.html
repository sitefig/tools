Conversion Rate Calculator (and Conversions Needed for a Target)

Enter visitors and conversions for the rate, and a target rate for what it takes to reach it.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

The rate is simple, but the target view is what helps planning: how many more conversions a 3% goal means on the same traffic, and how much traffic the current rate would need.

Define visitors the same way as your analytics tool (sessions or users), and conversions as one event, or the rate is not comparable between sources.

How to use it

  1. Enter visitors and conversions.
  2. Enter a target rate, or 0 to skip.
  3. Read the rate and the gap.
  4. Use the A/B tools to plan improvements.

Frequently asked questions

Visitors or sessions?

Use whichever your analytics reports, and stay consistent.

What is a good rate?

It varies a lot by site and goal; compare with your own history.

Can conversions exceed visitors?

Not here, since each visitor counts at most once.

Is data stored?

No.
