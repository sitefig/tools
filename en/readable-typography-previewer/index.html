Readable Typography Previewer: Font, Spacing and Line Length for Dyslexia

Previews text with typography settings often recommended for dyslexia and low vision.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Readers differ, and spacing and size often matter more than the font name.

Paste text and change font, size, letter and word spacing, line height, line length, colours and alignment. The preview updates, the text contrast is checked and the CSS is ready to copy. The advice is common guidance and research on special fonts is mixed; let users adjust text themselves.

How to use it

  1. Paste a sample of your text.
  2. Adjust font, spacing and colours.
  3. Check the contrast line.
  4. Copy the CSS.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a dyslexia font here?

No. Only installed system fonts are used.

What contrast is needed?

At least 4.5 to 1 for normal text (AA).

Why avoid justify?

It creates uneven gaps that make reading harder for many.

Is the text uploaded?

No.
