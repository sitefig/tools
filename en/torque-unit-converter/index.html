Torque Converter: Newton-Metres and Foot-Pounds

Converts torque between newton-metres, pound-force feet and inches, kilogram-force metres and centimetres, ounce-force inches and more.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Torque specs appear as N m in one manual and lbf ft in another. A wrong conversion can under-tighten a wheel or strip a thread.

This tool uses standard gravity and the exact pound, and shows all units at once. It does not know your fastener, so always follow the maker.

How to use it

  1. Enter the torque value.
  2. Choose the unit it is in.
  3. Read the other units.

Frequently asked questions

How many lbf ft is 100 N m?

About 73.76 lbf ft.

What is kgf m?

Kilogram-force metre, an older metric unit. 1 kgf m is 9.80665 N m.

Can I trust this for safety parts?

The maths is exact, but use the figure from your vehicle or part maker and a calibrated wrench.
