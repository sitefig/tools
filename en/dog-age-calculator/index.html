Dog Age in Human Years Calculator

See how old your dog is in human terms, with three different methods.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

The 2020 UCSD formula comes from DNA-methylation research on Labrador retrievers: human age = 16 ln(dog age) + 31 for dogs over a year old. The size-based rule counts 15 human years for the first year, 9 for the second and 4, 5 or 6 per year after that for small, medium and large dogs. The 7x rule is shown for comparison. A life stage label (puppy, young adult, adult, senior, geriatric) is added.

No formula is correct for every dog, because breeds age at different rates. Use it for fun and ask a vet about senior care.

No data is sent anywhere: the work happens in your browser, so you can use it offline once the page has loaded.

How to use it

  1. Enter the dog's age in years and months.
  2. Choose the size.
  3. Compare the three methods.
  4. Talk to your vet about age-appropriate care.

Frequently asked questions

Why different answers?

Each method is a model. The 2020 formula is research-based but derived from one breed.

When is a dog a senior?

Around 7 years, earlier for giant breeds.

Does this work for cats?

No, cats have different guidelines.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything is processed in your browser, and the page makes no network requests with your input.
