Scratchpad Notes

A simple notepad with multiple notes. Everything you type is saved in your browser's local storage on this device, with no account and no upload.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Everyone needs somewhere to park a snippet, a phone number, a half-written reply or a to-do list for a few minutes. Opening a full notes app, signing in or creating a document feels like too much when all you want is a blank box.

This scratchpad saves as you type, keeps several notes in a list on the left, and names each one after its first line. The notes live in your browser's local storage on this device only, not on a server. That makes them private, but also fragile: clearing site data, using a private window or switching browser loses them, and they do not sync between devices. Copy anything important somewhere safe.

How to use it

  1. Type into the note area; saving happens automatically.
  2. Press the add button to start a new note.
  3. Click a note in the list to switch to it.
  4. Copy important text elsewhere for a backup.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my notes stored?

In your browser's local storage for this site, on this device. They are never sent to a server. This also means nobody else can read them from here, and you cannot open them from another device.

When could I lose my notes?

If you clear site data or cookies, use a private or incognito window, or the browser blocks storage. A message says when storage is unavailable. Treat the scratchpad as temporary and copy anything you cannot lose.

Is there a size limit?

Browsers allow only a few megabytes of local storage per site, shared with other data this site stores. That is plenty for text notes, but not for pasted images or very large documents.

Can I use it offline?

Once the page has loaded, yes. Notes are saved locally and no connection is needed to type or save them.
