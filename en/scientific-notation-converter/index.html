Scientific and Engineering Notation Converter

Writes numbers as a coefficient times a power of ten (scientific and engineering), and expands notation such as 6.02e23 or 1.5 x 10^-3 into decimals.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Very large and very small numbers are easier to read as a power of ten. Calculators show 6.02E23 while a paper may say 6.02 x 10^23.

Digits are processed as text, so nothing is rounded. Engineering notation uses exponents that are multiples of three, matching SI prefixes.

How to use it

  1. Enter one number or notation per line.
  2. Read scientific, engineering and decimal forms.
  3. Copy what you need.

Frequently asked questions

What forms can I enter?

Plain numbers, 6.02e23, 6.02 x 10^23 and 6.02 times 10^23 with the times sign.

What is engineering notation?

Scientific notation where the exponent is a multiple of 3.

Does it track significant figures?

No. Trailing zeros are dropped.
