Email Drip Sequence Cadence Planner (Send Dates)

List your emails with delays and get a dated timeline.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Drip sequences are described in delays, but you schedule them on dates. Converting "3 days after the previous email" into real weekdays shows when a send lands on a weekend.

Each delay can count from signup, the previous email or a named earlier email. Times are in the zone you plan in. Turn on the weekend option to move Saturday and Sunday sends to Monday.

How to use it

  1. Choose the start date and send time.
  2. Write one email per line as Name | delay.
  3. Optionally move weekend sends to Monday.
  4. Copy the timeline into your tool.

Frequently asked questions

What delay formats work?

Numbers with h, d or w (hours, days, weeks), and an optional "after signup", "after previous" or "after Name".

Is a number alone allowed?

Yes, it means days after the previous email.

Does it change time zones?

No. Use the time zone planner for that.

Can it export?

Copy the text output.
