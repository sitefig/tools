Frequency Converter: Hz, kHz, MHz and RPM

Converts frequency and rotational speed between hertz, kHz, MHz, GHz, revolutions per minute and radians per second, and shows the period of one cycle.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Motors are rated in RPM, signals in Hz and clocks in MHz or GHz. 1 Hz is 60 RPM, which is easy to forget.

The tool also shows the period of one cycle so you can move between frequency and time.

How to use it

  1. Enter the value.
  2. Pick its unit.
  3. Read the other units and the period.

Frequently asked questions

How many RPM is 50 Hz?

3000 RPM, as 1 Hz equals 60 RPM.

What is rad/s?

Angular speed. One turn per second is 2 pi, about 6.283 rad/s.

Does it give motor shaft speed from mains frequency?

No. Pole count and slip change the real speed.
