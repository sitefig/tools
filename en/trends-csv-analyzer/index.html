Google Trends CSV Analyzer (Seasonality, Peak, Growth)

Understand a Google Trends export: when each term peaks, whether it is growing and how seasonal it is.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Google Trends exports relative interest (0 to 100) over time. This analyzer reads the CSV and, for each term, reports the peak and its date, the average, how the latest period compares with the one before, the direction of the linear trend and, with at least two years of monthly data, a seasonality profile showing the strongest and weakest months.

Trends rescales every export and shows interest relative to its own peak, never absolute search volume, so compare terms only inside the same export. The page cannot fetch Trends data; export the CSV from trends.google.com and paste it.

How to use it

  1. Export the CSV from Google Trends (download arrow on the chart).
  2. Paste it here.
  3. Use the seasonality profile to plan content calendars.

Frequently asked questions

Does it give search volume?

No. Trends only gives relative interest.

How much data is needed for seasonality?

At least 24 monthly points.

Can it compare terms?

Yes, if they are in the same export, since values share one scale.
