GTIN Check Digit Calculator (EAN-13, UPC-A, EAN-8, GTIN-14)

Computes the last digit of a barcode number or checks whether a full number is valid.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

A mistyped barcode number is a common cause of rejected listings. The last digit is a check digit calculated from the others with alternating weights of 3 and 1, so most single-digit errors are caught.

Paste numbers one per line. Seven or eleven digits get a check digit; eight, twelve, thirteen or fourteen digits are checked. A twelve digit number is shown both as an EAN-13 payload and as a full UPC-A. A valid check digit does not mean the number is assigned to your product.

How to use it

  1. Paste one number per line.
  2. Read the check digit or the validity of each.
  3. Fix the digits that fail and run again.

Frequently asked questions

Does a valid number mean the product is registered?

No. It only means the check digit fits. Numbers must be issued by GS1 or a reseller.

Why is a 12 digit number shown twice?

It could be the start of an EAN-13 or a complete UPC-A.

Can it draw the barcode?

Use the Barcode Generator for that.
