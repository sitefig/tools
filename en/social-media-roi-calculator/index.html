Social Media ROI Calculator: ROAS, Cost per Result and Break-Even

Work out the return on social ad spend, including tool and labour costs: ROI, ROAS, cost per click, cost per conversion and break-even revenue.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Ad platforms report return on ad spend, which ignores the tools, the creative and the hours it took. A campaign can look profitable on ROAS and lose money.

Enter ad spend, tool costs, hours and rate, other costs and the revenue you attribute to the campaign. The tool shows full-cost ROI, ROAS, cost per conversion, cost per click, conversion rate and break-even revenue. Set a margin if revenue is not profit.

How to use it

  1. Enter ad spend and the other costs.
  2. Enter revenue, conversions and clicks.
  3. Set a margin if revenue is not all profit.
  4. Read ROI, ROAS and break-even.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between ROI and ROAS?

ROAS is revenue divided by ad spend. ROI here is profit divided by all costs.

What revenue should I enter?

Revenue you can attribute to the campaign, which is the hard part.

Why a margin field?

ROI on revenue overstates success if the margin is thin.
