Heatmap Maker (Colour a Matrix by Value)

Shades every cell of a numeric grid by its value. Pick a colour scale, show or hide the numbers, and get a legend and a table.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

A heatmap helps you find the hot and cold cells in a cross-tab, a correlation matrix or a schedule. The numbers can stay in the cells so the picture never replaces the data.

Text inside each cell switches between dark and light for readable contrast. Scales include a sequential blue, viridis-style, heat, grey and a diverging blue-white-red scale centred on zero. Colour alone is a weak channel, so keep the values on for anything you must read exactly.

How to use it

  1. Paste a grid: first row column labels, first column row labels.
  2. Choose a colour scale; use diverging for values around zero.
  3. Show values in cells if the grid is small enough.
  4. Copy or download the SVG.

Frequently asked questions

Can I leave cells empty?

Yes, they are drawn grey as no value.

Is the scale linear?

Yes, from the smallest to the largest value, or symmetric around zero for the diverging scale.

How big a grid?

Up to 10000 cells.

Is it colour-blind safe?

The blue, viridis and grey scales are. The numbers in cells are the backup.
