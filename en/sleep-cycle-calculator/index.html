Sleep Cycle Calculator (Bedtime and Wake-Up Times)

Plan your sleep so you wake at the end of a cycle.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Enter a time and choose whether it is your wake-up time or bedtime. The page lists times for six, five, four and three 90-minute cycles after allowing the minutes you need to fall asleep (15 by default). Both the cycle length and the latency can be changed.

The 90-minute cycle is an average that varies between people and through the night. Adults generally need 7 to 9 hours, and this is a planning aid, not medical advice.

No data is sent anywhere: the work happens in your browser, so you can use it offline once the page has loaded.

How to use it

  1. Choose wake-up time or bedtime mode.
  2. Enter the time in 24-hour format.
  3. Adjust minutes to fall asleep.
  4. Pick a time that suits your schedule.

Frequently asked questions

Is 90 minutes right for me?

It is an average; individual cycles range from about 80 to 110 minutes.

How many cycles do I need?

Five to six cycles (7.5 to 9 hours) suit many adults.

Will it fix poor sleep?

No. Persistent problems are worth discussing with a clinician.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything is processed in your browser, and the page makes no network requests with your input.
