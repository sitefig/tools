Employee Total Cost Calculator (Salary, Employer Charges, Benefits, Overhead)

Adds employer charges, benefits, equipment and overhead to a salary to get the total yearly, monthly and hourly cost.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

A salary is only part of what an employee costs. Employer taxes and social charges, benefits, equipment, software and office space all add up, often to well over the salary.

Employer charges differ by country and change yearly, so no rate is built in. Enter your own from payroll. The tool also divides the total by productive days to get a day and hour cost.

How to use it

  1. Enter gross salary and the employer charge rate.
  2. Add benefits, equipment and overhead.
  3. Enter productive days and read the total and hourly cost.

Frequently asked questions

Which employer rate should I use?

Ask your payroll provider or tax authority.

What are productive days?

Working days after holidays and expected sick leave.

Is this a quote?

No, it is an estimate.
