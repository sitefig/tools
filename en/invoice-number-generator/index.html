Invoice Number Generator (Prefix, Year, Padding, Yearly Reset)

Next invoice numbers in your format, continuing from the last one you issued.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Invoice numbers must be unique and sequential in most countries. Typing them by hand invites duplicates and typos.

Set a prefix, separator, year style and padding. Paste the last number and the tool continues from it, or restarts at your start value when the year changes and you choose to reset.

How to use it

  1. Set prefix, separator, year style and digits.
  2. Paste your last number, or leave empty.
  3. Copy the numbers.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use several series?

Yes, use a different prefix for each series, for example credit notes.

Are gaps allowed?

Rules differ by country. Document any gap.

Does it store the counter?

No. Paste the last number each time.
