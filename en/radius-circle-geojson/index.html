Radius Circle Generator (GeoJSON Polygon and SVG Around a Point)

Creates accurate circles of given radii around a point as GeoJSON polygons, computed on the sphere.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

A radius around a shop, a site or an incident is a common need. This tool computes every point of the circle by travelling the radius on a great circle at equal bearings, so the circle is correct at any latitude, unlike a circle drawn in degrees.

You get GeoJSON ready for GIS tools or web maps, plus a preview with the radii labelled. Several radii make rings. The circle is a polygon with the number of points you choose; 72 is smooth for most uses.

How to use it

  1. Type the centre as lat, lon.
  2. List the radii, comma separated, and pick the unit.
  3. Set the number of points per circle.
  4. Copy or download the GeoJSON.

Frequently asked questions

Is the area exact?

It is the spherical cap area, not the polygon area.

Does it cross the date line?

Positions are normalised to -180..180, but a polygon crossing the line is not split.

Can I draw on a map?

No, there are no map tiles; the preview is a simple plan.

Maximum radius?

About 10000 km.
