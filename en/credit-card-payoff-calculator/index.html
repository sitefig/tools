Credit Card Payoff Calculator (Months to Zero, Interest, Payment Needed)

Shows the time to clear a credit card balance and the interest it costs, or the monthly payment needed to finish by a date.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Card interest compounds monthly, so paying only a little above the interest keeps you in debt for years. Seeing the months and the total interest at different payments makes the cost concrete.

Set the payment to 0 and give a target number of months to get the payment you need. Real cards compute interest daily and may change the rate, so use the result as an estimate.

How to use it

  1. Enter the balance and the APR.
  2. Enter a fixed monthly payment, or 0 and a target number of months.
  3. Read the months to zero and the total interest.

Frequently asked questions

Why do I get an error about interest?

If the payment does not cover the first month of interest, the balance never goes down. Raise the payment.

Does it include new purchases?

No. It assumes you stop using the card.

Is daily interest modelled?

No. It uses APR divided by 12 each month.
