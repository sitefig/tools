Mileage Allowance Calculator (Business Kilometres at Your Rate)

Reimbursement for business kilometres at rates that you enter.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Many employers and tax authorities set per-km rates, sometimes lower after a distance. Enter your rate and the tool does the arithmetic and checks an optional annual cap.

No official rates are bundled because they change by country and year.

How to use it

  1. Enter kilometres and the rate.
  2. Optionally set a second rate and distance.
  3. Optionally enter a cap.

Frequently asked questions

Which rate should I use?

The current figure from your tax authority or employer policy.

What records do I need?

Date, route, purpose and odometer readings.

Is it taxed?

Rules differ by country.
