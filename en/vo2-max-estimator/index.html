VO2 Max Estimator (Cooper 12-Minute Test or Heart Rate Ratio)

Estimate VO2 max from a Cooper run or from heart rates.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Pick a method. The Cooper test uses the distance you cover in 12 minutes of hard running on flat ground: VO2 max = (metres - 504.9) / 44.73. The heart rate method uses 15.3 x max heart rate / resting heart rate (Uth et al.).

Both are field estimates. The heart rate version was fitted on well-trained men, and it is weaker when max heart rate is a guess. The page does not rank the result by age and sex, because the norm tables could not be checked against a source.

How to use it

  1. Choose Cooper or the heart rate method.
  2. Enter the distance or the two heart rates.
  3. Read the estimate in ml/kg/min.
  4. Retest under the same conditions to track change.

Frequently asked questions

Why no age and sex ranking?

Published norm tables differ and could not be verified here, so the tool leaves them out rather than guess.

Is a watch estimate better?

Watches use more inputs but are also estimates. Lab testing is the reference.

Is this medical advice?

No. It is an estimate from a published formula. A doctor or dietitian can judge your own case far better.

Is anything uploaded?

No. The work happens in your browser and the page makes no network request with your input.
