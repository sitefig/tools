Business Card Designer: Print-Ready PDF with Bleed and Crop Marks

Fills in a business card layout and creates a PDF with the finished card size, the bleed area, crop marks and the TrimBox and BleedBox set for your print shop.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Print shops want the background to run past the cut line (bleed) and marks showing where to cut. This tool extends the background into the bleed you set, draws marks outside it and writes the PDF boxes. Three layouts: left aligned, accent bar and bottom band.

It uses the standard Helvetica fonts, so only Latin text prints correctly, and colours are RGB. Ask your printer if they need CMYK or specific bleed, and keep text 3 mm inside the cut. Order a proof first.

How to use it

  1. Enter your name, title and contact details.
  2. Pick the card size, layout, bleed and colours.
  3. Run the tool and open the PDF to check it.
  4. Send it to your print shop.

Frequently asked questions

What bleed should I use?

Three millimetres is common, but your printer decides.

Is it CMYK?

No. The PDF uses RGB colours. Ask your printer about conversion.

Why do some characters show as ?

Only Latin-1 characters work with the built-in fonts.

Can I add a logo?

Not in this version.
