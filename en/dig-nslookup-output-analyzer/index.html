dig and nslookup Output Analyzer (MX, NS, TXT, SPF, DMARC, DKIM)

Paste what dig or nslookup printed and get the records grouped, explained and checked for common DNS and email authentication mistakes.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

DNS debugging usually starts with dig. This tool parses its output (full, +short and the common nslookup layouts, several queries at once) and reports what matters: NXDOMAIN or SERVFAIL meaning, SPF terms with a rough count of the 10-lookup limit, a second SPF record, +all policies, DMARC p= and rua= settings, DKIM key length estimates, MX priorities and IP targets, fewer than two name servers, CNAMEs that coexist with other records, TTL range and DNSSEC signs.

The page makes no DNS queries; the real answers come from your own resolver, so paste the output. The SPF count cannot follow nested includes, and cached answers show remaining TTL, not the configured value.

How to use it

  1. Run dig (for example `dig example.com MX TXT +noall +answer +comments`) and copy the output.
  2. Paste it; multiple queries in one paste are fine.
  3. Read the grouped records and the checks section.

Frequently asked questions

Why can it not just query my domain?

Browsers cannot send raw DNS queries and this site never makes network requests from tools. Pasting dig output keeps it private and works for internal zones too.

Does it follow SPF includes?

No. It counts the lookup mechanisms in the record you pasted; paste the included records or use the SPF flattener for the full tree.

Which formats are supported?

dig full output, dig +short and the common nslookup output.
