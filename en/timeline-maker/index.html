Timeline Maker (Dated Events on a Line, SVG)

Places dated events on a line with their titles and descriptions. Dates can be a year, a year-month or a full day.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

A timeline turns a list of dates into a story. Paste the events and get a clean line, alternating above and below to avoid overlap.

Even spacing keeps the text readable; to scale shows the real gaps, which is more honest but crowds close events. Dates are sorted for you. The event table below the image gives a text version.

How to use it

  1. Write one event per line: date | title | description.
  2. Choose horizontal or vertical, even or to scale.
  3. Check the table for order.
  4. Copy or download the SVG.

Frequently asked questions

Which date formats?

YYYY, YYYY-MM and YYYY-MM-DD, with / allowed in full dates.

BC dates?

Years from -999 are accepted for the year form.

Why is a description cut?

Long descriptions are limited to a few lines.

Limit?

60 events.
