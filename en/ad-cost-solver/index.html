CPM, CPC and CPA Solver (Fill Any Two, Get the Third)

Fill two of the three values in a group and the calculator solves for the third.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Ad costs are three related numbers: spend, units and cost per unit. Often you know two and need the third, for example how many clicks a budget buys at a given CPC.

Leave a field empty to mark it unknown. CTR and click-to-conversion rates are shown when the numbers are present. CPM is cost per 1000 impressions.

How to use it

  1. Enter the spend.
  2. In each group fill the units or the rate, leaving the one you want empty.
  3. Read the solved values.
  4. Check for the agreement warning when all three are filled.

Frequently asked questions

What is CPM?

Cost per thousand impressions.

Can I solve for spend?

Yes, leave spend empty and fill units and rate.

Why is only one group solved?

Each group needs two known values.

Is data stored?

No.
