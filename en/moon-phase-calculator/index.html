Moon Phase Calculator: Phase, Illumination and Next Full Moon

Gives moon phase name, illumination, age and the next full and new moons for a date, and a day-by-day table for the month.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Look up the phase for a birthday, a photo shoot or a garden plan. New and full moon times use the main terms of Meeus's method.

Checked against the ephem library: moon event times within 2 minutes on three cases, illumination within about 5 points. Dates are UTC and the local date may differ.

How to use it

  1. Pick a date.
  2. Tick Show every day of the month if you want the table.
  3. Read the phase, illumination and next moons.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate are the times?

Within a few minutes of the ephem library in my checks.

Is the illumination exact?

No, within about 5 percentage points.

Which time zone?

UTC, evaluated at noon UTC for the date.
