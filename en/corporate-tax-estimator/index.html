Corporate Tax Estimator (Flat or Two-Tier Rate)

Corporate tax, effective rate and optional dividend tax from your own rates.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Some countries tax small profits at a lower rate. Enter a reduced rate and the profit limit it applies to, plus any losses carried forward.

An optional dividend step shows the combined burden on distributed profit. Taxable profit is accounting profit adjusted by many rules that are not modelled. Not tax advice. Check national rules.

How to use it

  1. Enter profit, losses and the standard rate.
  2. Optionally add a reduced rate and limit.
  3. Optionally add the dividend share and tax.

Frequently asked questions

Which rate should I use?

Your country's current rate from the tax authority.

Are surtaxes included?

No.

Does it compute taxable profit?

No. You enter it.
