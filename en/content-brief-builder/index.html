Content Brief Builder from Competitor Headings

Turns the headings of competing pages into a brief: topics most pages cover, questions asked, common terms and a draft outline for your own page.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Writers often start from an empty page. Seeing what the top pages cover, and which questions they answer, gives a starting outline in minutes.

Paste each competitor's headings (Markdown, HTML or lines like H2: Title) and separate pages with a line of three equal signs. Topics are grouped by shared words in the headings, so check the groups. A brief shows what others cover. It cannot tell you what will rank or what your own angle should be.

How to use it

  1. Type the target keyword in the first box.
  2. Paste the headings of each competitor page, separated by ===.
  3. Read the topics, questions and terms.
  4. Edit the draft outline and add your own expertise.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get headings from a page?

View the page source or use a heading extractor, then paste the h1 to h4 lines. Markdown # headings also work.

How many pages should I use?

Three to ten of the top results is enough.

Does it copy competitors?

It only counts topics. Write your own text and add information they do not have.

Can it fetch the pages?

No. A web page cannot fetch other sites, so you paste the headings.
