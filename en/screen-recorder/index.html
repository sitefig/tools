Screen Recorder

Record your whole screen, a single window or a browser tab to a WebM file, with an option to include audio. The recording stays on your device.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Bug reports, tutorials and quick walkthroughs are much clearer as a short recording than as a paragraph of steps. Installing a desktop recorder for a one-off clip is overkill, and many web recorders upload your screen to their servers, which is unwelcome when it shows internal tools or customer data.

This recorder uses the screen capture feature built into your browser. You choose what to share, record, stop and get a preview you can play right away and download as a WebM file. The video is assembled in memory on your device and never sent anywhere. Audio capture depends on your browser and operating system: tab audio is widely supported, while system audio is not available everywhere. Long recordings use a lot of memory.

How to use it

  1. Tick or untick Include audio.
  2. Press Start recording and choose a screen, window or tab in the browser prompt.
  3. Press Stop when you have finished, or end sharing from the browser bar.
  4. Play the preview and press Download WebM.

Frequently asked questions

Is my recording uploaded?

No. The video is created by your browser and stored in memory until you download it. Nothing is sent to a server. Closing the page discards any recording you did not save.

Why is there no sound?

Browsers differ in what audio they can capture. Tab audio often works, whole-screen system audio only on some systems, and microphone audio is not included by this tool. Check the share prompt for an audio checkbox.

What format is the file?

WebM, using VP9 or VP8 video with Opus audio where the browser supports them. Most players handle it. Convert to MP4 with a desktop tool if a specific app needs that format.

Is there a time limit?

No fixed limit, but the recording is held in memory, so very long clips can slow down or crash the tab. Record in shorter segments for lengthy sessions.
