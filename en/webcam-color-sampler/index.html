Webcam Color Sampler: Read an Object Color Through the Camera

Shows the live camera view with a sampling box and reports the average colour inside it as HEX and RGB. Save colours into a list.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Matching a paint chip or a fabric starts with a number. The tool averages a small box of pixels, which reduces camera noise. Move the box with sliders and set its size.

Camera auto white balance and lighting change what is measured, so use a neutral light and compare with a known reference. The video stays in your browser.

How to use it

  1. Press Start camera and allow access.
  2. Move the box over your object with the position sliders.
  3. Press Save this colour to keep a value.
  4. Click a saved colour to copy it.

Frequently asked questions

Is the colour accurate?

It is a guide. Cameras adjust colour automatically and lights tint objects.

Does it save video?

No. Frames are only drawn on the screen.

Can I sample a photo?

Use the Color Picker from Image.

Why a box and not one pixel?

Averaging cuts noise.
