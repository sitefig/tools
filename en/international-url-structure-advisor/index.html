ccTLD vs Subdomain vs Subfolder Advisor

A simple scoring helper for the first decision in international SEO: how to structure URLs for different markets.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Google lists the pros and cons of country-code domains, subdomains and subdirectories in its multi-regional site guidance. This tool weighs those points against your answers on markets, team size, per-country targeting, hosting needs and brand awareness, and prints the ranked options with the reasons.

The weights are a rule of thumb, not research. Google does not say how authority carries between hosts, so the tool does not claim that. Whatever you choose, hreflang between versions is still needed.

How to use it

  1. Choose your answers in the options.
  2. Read the ranked list and the reasons.
  3. Compare with Google's trade-offs listed below it.
  4. Decide, then plan redirects and hreflang.

Frequently asked questions

Is the ranking reliable?

It is a rule of thumb with simple weights. Treat it as a discussion starter.

Why not parameters?

Google lists URL parameters as not recommended.

What about the .eu domain?

Google treats .eu as a generic domain, so it gives no country signal.

Does hosting location matter?

Google says server location is a weak signal, but subfolders share one server location.
