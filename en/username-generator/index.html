Username Generator (Gaming, Discord, Reddit, Twitch)

Get a list of usable handles and shortlist the ones you like.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Pick a style (CamelCase, snake_case, dashes, lowercase or noun plus number), optionally a platform whose published length and character limits are applied (Discord 2-32, Reddit 3-20 letters, digits, _ and -, Twitch 4-25 letters, digits and _), your own keyword and whether to add digits. Names are random combinations of 30 adjectives and 30 nouns with secure randomness.

The names are random, so availability must be checked on the platform. Rules shown are the commonly published limits and can change.

No data is sent anywhere: the work happens in your browser, so you can use it offline once the page has loaded.

How to use it

  1. Choose a platform and style.
  2. Add a keyword if you want it included.
  3. Generate and note favourites.
  4. Check availability on the site.

Frequently asked questions

Are these names taken?

Possibly. The tool cannot check availability.

Can I use my name?

Enter it as the keyword to mix it into some results.

Do names include digits?

Optionally, to improve the chance of finding a free one.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything is processed in your browser, and the page makes no network requests with your input.
