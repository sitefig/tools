Social Post Planner: Weekly Calendar and CSV Export

Paste your planned posts (date, time, platform, text) and get them sorted into a weekly calendar or a CSV, with clashes and bad dates flagged.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

A content plan often starts in a notes file. Putting it in order by hand invites errors, such as two posts at the same time.

Write one line per post: date and time, platform and text, separated by bars. The tool sorts them, groups them by week (Monday first), flags clashes and lines it cannot read, and shows counts per platform. Switch to CSV for a spreadsheet. Times are not converted between time zones.

How to use it

  1. Write one line per post: 2026-03-03 09:00 | LinkedIn | Text.
  2. Choose calendar or CSV.
  3. Fix any flagged lines.
  4. Copy the plan.

Frequently asked questions

Does it schedule posts?

No. It organises a plan. Posting needs your platform or a scheduler.

What about time zones?

Times are kept as written.

How is the CSV quoted?

Platform and text are in quotes with doubled inner quotes.
