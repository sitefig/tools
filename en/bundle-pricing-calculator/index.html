Bundle Pricing Calculator (Discount Against Item Margins)

Shows what a bundle discount does to profit and the biggest discount that still meets a margin target.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

A bundle discount is a cost: you give away part of the price on items that might have sold anyway. It pays off only when it sells extra items or lifts basket size.

List items with cost and price. The tool shows the margin of each, the profit sold separately and as a bundle, and the largest discount that keeps a target margin on the whole bundle.

How to use it

  1. List items as name, cost, price.
  2. Set the bundle discount and your target margin.
  3. Compare profit and read the maximum discount.

Frequently asked questions

How is the maximum discount computed?

It is the largest discount where bundle price still gives at least the target margin on total cost.

Does it model extra sales?

No. Estimate those separately.

What about different tax rates per item?

Use prices without tax.
