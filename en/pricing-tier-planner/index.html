Pricing Tier Planner (Good-Better-Best Revenue and Margin)

Compares up to a handful of plans on revenue, margin and customer mix.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Tiered pricing works when each plan has its own audience. The numbers to check are revenue from each plan, margin per customer and the mix, because a cheap plan can make most of the customers and little of the profit.

List plans as name, price, expected customers and cost to serve. Customer counts are your forecast, so test variations.

How to use it

  1. List plans as name, price, customers, cost.
  2. Read revenue, margin and mix per plan.
  3. Change counts to try different scenarios.

Frequently asked questions

What is cost to serve?

Variable cost per customer, like support, hosting and payment fees.

Can I compare monthly and yearly?

Use the same period for price and cost.

Does it model upgrades?

No. Adjust the customer counts instead.
