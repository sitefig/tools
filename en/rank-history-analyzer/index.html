Keyword Rank History Analyzer (Movers, Volatility, Top-10 Share)

Analyse rank tracking exports: who gained or lost, how volatile each keyword is and when everything moved at once.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Rank trackers export long tables that hide the story. This tool takes either a long format (date, keyword, position) or a wide one (keyword and one column per date) and reports per-keyword first, last, change, best, worst and standard deviation, the keywords that entered or left the top 3 and top 10, the top-10 share at start and end, and the average absolute position change per period, where spikes often match algorithm updates.

Rank data is sampled from one location and device, so day-to-day noise of a few places is normal. Compare spikes with known update dates before drawing conclusions.

How to use it

  1. Export your tracker data as CSV.
  2. Paste it (long or wide format).
  3. Review movers, volatility and the turbulence table.

Frequently asked questions

What is volatility here?

The standard deviation of the positions over the period; higher means a less stable ranking.

Can it tell me about the whole SERP?

No. It sees only the positions of your own tracked keywords.

Which formats are accepted?

date,keyword,position rows, or keyword plus date columns.
