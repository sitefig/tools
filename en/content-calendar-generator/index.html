Content Calendar Generator

Enter your topics, a start date and a cadence, and get a dated content calendar as a Markdown table or CSV, ready to paste into a doc or spreadsheet.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

A list of article ideas is not a plan until each one has a date. Laying them out by hand in a spreadsheet is tedious, and it gets worse when you want weekdays only, or a monthly slot that must handle short months.

This generator takes your topics in order and assigns each a date from your start date at the cadence you choose: every day, weekdays only, weekly, every two weeks or monthly. The output has date, weekday, topic, channel and a Planned status, in Markdown for wikis and pull requests, or CSV for spreadsheets. The dates come from simple calendar rules, and nothing is stored or uploaded.

How to use it

  1. Enter your topics, one per line, in the order you want to publish them.
  2. Set the start date as YYYY-MM-DD, the cadence and the channel name.
  3. Choose the output format: markdown or csv.
  4. Copy the calendar into your document, board or spreadsheet.

Frequently asked questions

How does the monthly cadence handle short months?

It keeps the same day of the month where possible. If the start date is the 31st, shorter months use their last day, such as 28 or 30 February.

What does the weekdays cadence do?

It schedules one topic per working day, skipping Saturdays and Sundays. If your start date falls on a weekend, the first topic moves to the following Monday.

Does it know about public holidays?

No. It only skips weekends, and only in weekdays mode. Adjust dates by hand around holidays that matter to your audience.

Why is the date format YYYY-MM-DD?

It is unambiguous, sorts correctly and imports cleanly into spreadsheets. A start date in another format is rejected with a message asking for an example like 2026-01-05.
