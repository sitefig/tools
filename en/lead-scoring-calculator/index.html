Lead Scoring Calculator (Hot, Warm, Cold)

Enter criteria with points and the criteria each lead meets to get a score and label.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Lead scoring turns "this lead looks good" into a number you can sort by. Fit signals such as company size get points, behaviour signals such as visiting pricing get points, and bad signals can subtract.

The weights here are yours, not research. Calibrate them against leads that became customers. Unknown criteria in the lead lines are listed and ignored.

How to use it

  1. List criteria as name: points.
  2. List each lead with the criteria it meets, separated by semicolons.
  3. Set the hot and warm thresholds.
  4. Read the ranked leads.
