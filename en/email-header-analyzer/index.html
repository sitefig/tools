Email Header Analyzer (Received Path, SPF, DKIM, DMARC)

Find out where a message came from, how long each hop took and whether it passed authentication.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

The analyzer lists the Received chain oldest first with from/by hosts, protocols and timestamps, computes the delay between hops (flagging slow hops and clock skew), reads the Authentication-Results header for SPF, DKIM and DMARC, shows DKIM signature domains and selectors, compares the From and Return-Path domains and the DKIM domain for alignment, and notes a different Reply-To.

Headers added below your own server's lines can be forged, so trust only the Received and Authentication-Results lines added by servers you control. It does not query DNS to verify records.

No data is sent anywhere: the work happens in your browser, so you can use it offline once the page has loaded.

How to use it

  1. In your mail client open "Show original" or "View headers".
  2. Copy all header lines and paste them.
  3. Read the route and authentication.
  4. Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC DNS records if something fails.

Frequently asked questions

What is DMARC alignment?

The domain in From must match the domain authenticated by SPF or DKIM.

Why is a hop slow?

Greylisting, queueing or scanning. Over five minutes is flagged.

Can I trust the first Received line?

The topmost line is added by your own server; lines further down can be forged.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything is processed in your browser, and the page makes no network requests with your input.
