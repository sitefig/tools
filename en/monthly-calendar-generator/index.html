Printable Monthly Calendar Generator with Marked Dates

Creates a print-ready monthly or yearly calendar with Monday or Sunday start, ISO week numbers and highlighted dates with labels.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

The existing yearly calendar fits a year on one page. This one gives each month a full page with room to write, and lets you mark dates such as holidays or deadlines.

Paste dates with labels, choose the month or the whole year, then print or save as PDF from your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

How to use it

  1. Choose the year and month.
  2. Add marked dates and labels.
  3. Print or save as PDF from the preview.

Frequently asked questions

Can I print all twelve months?

Yes, choose Whole year for one page per month.

How are week numbers counted?

ISO 8601 weeks, which start on Monday.

Does it include public holidays?

No. Add the ones you need as marked dates.
