Email Send Time Zone Planner (Local Arrival Times)

Enter a send date and time in your zone and a list of subscriber zones to see when it lands for each.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

A 9:00 send in Brussels arrives at 4:00 in New York. For international lists, a quick table of local times shows which regions get the email overnight and which in working hours.

Daylight saving is applied using the date you choose, through the browser's built-in time zone data. Zones need IANA names such as Europe/Paris. Arrival is not opening: people read when they want to.

How to use it

  1. Enter your time zone, send date and time.
  2. List subscriber time zones, one per line.
  3. Set what counts as overnight.
  4. Read the local times and flags.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find zone names?

Use IANA names like America/Chicago. Optional labels go before a pipe.

Does it handle daylight saving?

Yes, for the date you give.

What if the time does not exist on DST day?

The result shifts by the offset change.

Is anything uploaded?

No.
