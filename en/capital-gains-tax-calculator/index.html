Capital Gains Tax Calculator (Holding Period, Exemption and Losses)

Gain, taxable gain and tax from the rates and thresholds you enter.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Capital gains rules vary: some countries tax at income rates, some at flat rates, some exempt long holdings. This tool uses the values you enter, so it fits many systems.

It subtracts costs, applies losses and an exempt amount, and picks the long or short rate by days held. Not tax advice. Check national rules.

How to use it

  1. Enter purchase, sale, costs and days held.
  2. Enter your rates, threshold, exemption and losses.
  3. Read the gain and tax.

Frequently asked questions

Are rates built in?

No, you enter them.

Does it do FIFO lots?

No. See the crypto and shares calculator.

What if I have a loss?

It shows the loss and no tax.
