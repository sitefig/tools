Chinese Zodiac Animal and Element Finder by Birth Date

Gives the zodiac animal, element and polarity for a birth date, using the Chinese calendar data in your browser for New Year.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

People born in January or early February belong to the previous zodiac year. The tool finds Chinese New Year for the birth year and uses it.

Results matched known cases (1988 Dragon after February 17, 2024 Wood Dragon on February 10). Dates are taken in UTC, so New Year's Day itself may differ by time zone.

How to use it

  1. Enter the birth date.
  2. Read the animal and element.
  3. Check the New Year date shown.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my animal different from my birth year?

If you were born before Chinese New Year, your sign is the previous year's.

Is it astrology?

It is only the calendar lookup, with no interpretation.

Which years work?

1900 to 2100.
