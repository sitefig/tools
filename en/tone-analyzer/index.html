Tone of Voice Analyzer (Lexicon Based)

Check how your copy sounds: formal or casual, confident or hedging, urgent or calm, and whether it speaks to the reader.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Five dimensions are scored 0-100 from counts of formal and casual words, hedges and certainty words, urgency phrases, positive and negative words, second-person pronouns, exclamation marks, contractions, questions and average sentence length. The detail lines show the raw counts so you can see why a score came out as it did.

This is a lexicon method, so sarcasm, jargon and non-English text are misread. It is best for comparing two drafts of the same piece or checking that a brand voice guide is followed, not for judging a person.

No data is sent anywhere: the work happens in your browser, so you can use it offline once the page has loaded.

How to use it

  1. Paste at least a paragraph of English text.
  2. Read the five scores and the matched-word counts.
  3. Edit the draft and run it again to compare.
  4. Keep the tone that fits your audience.

Frequently asked questions

Is this machine learning?

No. It uses fixed word lists and punctuation counts.

What is a good formality score?

It depends on the audience: legal copy scores high, social posts low. Compare against your own examples.

Why is my score low on confidence?

Words like maybe, perhaps and might lower it; words like always, will and guarantee raise it.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything is processed in your browser, and the page makes no network requests with your input.
