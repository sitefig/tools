Square Footage / Area Calculator (Shapes)

Measure a room, lot or surface and get the area in the unit you need.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Pick a shape (rectangle, triangle, circle, trapezoid, ellipse or L-shape) and enter its dimensions; the page returns the area and perimeter in your unit and converts to square feet, square metres and square yards. The labels explain what each of A to D means for the chosen shape.

For flooring, paint or tiles add 5 to 10% for waste and cuts, and measure irregular rooms by splitting them into simple shapes.

No data is sent anywhere: the work happens in your browser, so you can use it offline once the page has loaded.

How to use it

  1. Select the shape and unit.
  2. Enter the dimensions A to D as described.
  3. Read the area and perimeter.
  4. Add a waste allowance when buying materials.

Frequently asked questions

How do I measure an L-shaped room?

Use the L-shape option: the bounding rectangle minus the cut-out corner.

What is the formula for a circle?

Area = pi times radius squared.

Is the perimeter of an ellipse exact?

It uses Ramanujan's close approximation.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything is processed in your browser, and the page makes no network requests with your input.
