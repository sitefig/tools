Accessibility Statement Generator (Web Accessibility Directive Style)

Generates an accessibility statement from your details with the usual EU-style sections.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Many public bodies must publish an accessibility statement, and other sites benefit from one.

The tool writes compliance status, known non-accessible content, preparation, feedback and optional enforcement sections. It refuses a partial or non-compliant statement with no listed issues. The structure was not checked against the EU Implementing Decision text in this session; verify it for your country.

How to use it

  1. Enter organisation, site and standard.
  2. List known issues one per line.
  3. Add contact and enforcement details.
  4. Review the drafting notes before publishing.

Frequently asked questions

Is it legally valid?

It is a template. Check national rules or ask a lawyer.

Can I claim full compliance?

Only with evidence for every criterion at the level.

Where do I publish it?

Link it from every page, for public bodies.

Is the data uploaded?

No.
