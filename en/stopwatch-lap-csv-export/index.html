Stopwatch with Laps and CSV Export

A stopwatch with start, stop, lap and reset, and a button to download all laps as a CSV file.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Timing a run, a speech or a test is more useful when the laps are saved. The CSV has lap number, split and total in mm:ss.hh and in seconds.

It uses a high-resolution browser clock. Nothing is uploaded.

How to use it

  1. Press Start.
  2. Press Lap at each split.
  3. Press Download CSV when finished.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is it?

Display updates every 50 ms; the clock itself is more precise. Browser tabs in the background may update less often.

Where is the file saved?

Your browser downloads folder as laps.csv.

Is anything stored?

No, laps vanish when you leave the page.
