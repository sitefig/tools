Oven Temperature Converter: Gas Mark, Celsius, Fan and Fahrenheit

Converts recipe oven temperatures between gas mark, conventional Celsius, fan-oven Celsius and Fahrenheit.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

UK recipes use gas marks, US recipes Fahrenheit and many ovens are fan-assisted. A fan oven is usually set about 20 C lower.

Enter any one setting and see the others. Gas marks follow the usual chart, which differs by a few degrees between sources.

How to use it

  1. Enter the setting from the recipe.
  2. Choose what kind of setting it is.
  3. Read the settings for your oven.

Frequently asked questions

What is gas mark 4 in Celsius?

180 C, which is 350 F, or 160 C in a fan oven.

Is the fan rule exact?

No. 20 C is a common rule. Some makers advise 10 to 25 C.

Can I enter 1/4 or 1/2?

Yes, as text, with the Gas mark option.
