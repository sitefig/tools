Sitemap Lastmod Timeline

Plots how many URLs were modified in each period and shows staleness by folder.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

A spike on one day often means the CMS rewrites every date on deploy. A folder whose newest date is two years old may be dead content.

The tool buckets valid lastmod values, flags a day holding half or more of the URLs, and compares folders. Set the reference date to repeat a report on a saved file.

How to use it

  1. Paste the sitemap XML.
  2. Pick day, month or year buckets, or leave auto.
  3. Set what counts as stale.
  4. Read the chart and the folder table.

Frequently asked questions

What is a spike?

One day with at least half the dated URLs.

Is a stale URL bad?

Not always. Evergreen pages may be fine; review them.

Which dates count?

Valid W3C dates only. Others are counted as missing.

Is this a crawl?

No. It only reads the file.
