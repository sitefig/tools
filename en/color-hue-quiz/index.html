Color Hue Quiz: Find the Odd Swatch

Shows nine swatches, one slightly different in lightness. Each correct pick makes the next difference smaller, up to 12 rounds.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

The quiz is built for fun and a rough feel for your colour vision on your own screen. Differences are measured in OKLab lightness, so steps are perceptually even. You get a score and the smallest difference you spotted.

It is not a medical test. Screen settings and room lighting change the result, and the game needs colour vision to play, so it has no non-visual mode.

How to use it

  1. Press Start new game.
  2. Pick the swatch that looks different.
  3. Continue through the rounds.
  4. Read your score and smallest difference.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a colour blindness test?

No. Use a clinical test for that. The Colour Blindness Simulator shows how others see colours.

What do the difference numbers mean?

OKLab lightness points on a 0 to 1 scale; lower means subtler.

Can I use the keyboard?

Yes. Every swatch is a button with a name like Swatch 3 of 9.

Why three misses?

To end the game quickly when the differences are below what you can see.
