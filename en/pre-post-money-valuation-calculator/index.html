Pre-Money and Post-Money Valuation Calculator

Solves for the missing one of pre-money valuation, investment and post-money valuation and shows ownership.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Post-money is pre-money plus the new investment. Investors own investment divided by post-money, and existing holders keep pre-money divided by post-money.

Leave one field at 0 and fill in the other two. The tool checks that the numbers make sense, for example that investment is smaller than post-money.

How to use it

  1. Enter two known values.
  2. Leave the unknown at 0.
  3. Read the third value and the ownership split.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between pre- and post-money?

Pre-money is value before new cash, post-money is value after.

Does it include option pool?

No. Use the cap table simulator.

Why must exactly two be filled?

Two values fix the third.
