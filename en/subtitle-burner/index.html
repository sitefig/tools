Subtitle Burner: Hardcode SRT or VTT Captions into a Video

Reads an SRT or VTT file and draws each caption onto the video frames at the right times, so they show on any player or platform.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Some platforms ignore subtitle files or show them badly. Burning the text into the picture guarantees it is seen. The tool wraps long lines, can draw a dark box behind them and places them at the bottom, middle or top.

Nothing is uploaded. The file is decoded by your browser and re-encoded, so quality drops a little and the result is WebM (or MP4 where your browser can record it). It records in real time, so a 60 second clip takes about a minute and the tab must stay visible. Hardcoded subtitles cannot be turned off or translated later, so keep your original video and the SRT.

How to use it

  1. Choose the video and the subtitle file.
  2. Set size, colour, box and position.
  3. Process and watch the progress.
  4. Check the result and download it.

Frequently asked questions

Which subtitle formats work?

SubRip (.srt) and WebVTT (.vtt) with standard time codes.

Can I use other fonts?

The three generic families (sans-serif, serif, monospace) are offered because they exist on every device.

Are non-English letters supported?

Yes, as long as your device font has them.

How do I fix timing first?

Use the Subtitle Timing Shifter, then burn.
