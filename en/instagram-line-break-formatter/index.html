Instagram Caption Line Break Formatter

Keep blank lines and spacing in captions and bios by filling empty lines with an invisible character. Shows the character count.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Captions and bios can lose blank lines or leading spaces when saved. A common workaround is a line holding an invisible character.

This tool puts the Braille blank, zero-width space or word joiner on each empty line and protects indents. It shows the new character count. Invisible characters can trouble screen readers, and Instagram has changed its handling before, so paste it in, look at the preview and keep the plain text as a fallback.

How to use it

  1. Paste your caption.
  2. Choose the invisible character.
  3. Decide whether to protect indents.
  4. Paste the result into the app and check the preview.

Frequently asked questions

Does it always work?

Not guaranteed. The platform's behaviour changes, and this was not tested against the current app.

Is it bad for accessibility?

It can be. Some screen readers announce the Braille blank. Use it sparingly.

Do invisible characters count?

Yes, each counts as a character.
