Video Looper and Boomerang Maker

Makes a longer clip by repeating a short segment, or a boomerang that plays forward and then backward.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Short loops suit social posts and website backgrounds. Repeat mode plays the segment again and again. Boomerang mode reads the frames into memory, then plays them forward and back, which is why it is limited to 10 seconds and a modest width.

Loop clips have no audio. It records in real time, so a 60 second clip takes about a minute and the tab must stay visible.

How to use it

  1. Choose the video and set the start and end (max 10 s).
  2. Pick Repeat or Boomerang and how many cycles.
  3. For a boomerang, set the frame rate and width.
  4. Process and download.

Frequently asked questions

Why no sound?

Reversing audio is not supported, and loops are usually silent.

Is the loop seamless?

Boomerang is seamless by design. For Repeat the cut point depends on your clip.

Why a 10 second limit?

Frames are kept in memory for boomerangs.

Can I make a GIF?

Use Video to GIF for that.
