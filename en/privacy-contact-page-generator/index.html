Privacy Contact Page and privacy.txt Generator

Generates a privacy contact page in HTML and a small text file with your privacy contacts.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

People who want to use their data rights need to find the right contact quickly. A dedicated page with the DPO, email, postal address and request form helps them, and so does a machine-readable file.

This tool builds both. The text file copies the look of security.txt but no standard defines a privacy.txt, so browsers and regulators do not read it automatically. Keep the page and your privacy policy consistent.

How to use it

  1. Fill in the organisation and contact details.
  2. Copy the HTML into a page on your site.
  3. Publish the text file at /.well-known/privacy.txt if you want to.
  4. Link the page from your privacy policy and footer.

Frequently asked questions

Is privacy.txt a standard?

No. It is an informal convention, modelled on security.txt (RFC 9116).

Is a DPO required?

Only in some cases (GDPR Art. 37). Leave the field empty if you have none.

Where should I link the page?

In the footer and the privacy policy.
