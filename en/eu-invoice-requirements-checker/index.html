EU Invoice Requirements Checker (VAT Directive Article 226)

A checklist of the 15 details Article 226 lists for VAT invoices, adjusted to your case.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Article 226 of Directive 2006/112/EC lists the only details required for VAT purposes: date, sequential number, VAT IDs, names and addresses, description, supply date, taxable amount, rate, VAT amount and special references.

Tick the special cases that apply and what your invoice already shows. Items that do not apply are listed separately. Member states may add national rules such as e-invoicing, so this is a minimum check.

How to use it

  1. Tick the cases that apply, such as reverse charge.
  2. Tick each detail your invoice shows.
  3. Read the missing list.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the full legal requirement?

It is the EU-level list. Your country may require more or allow simplified invoices.

Why is the supply date optional?

It is needed only when it differs from the issue date.

Does it read my invoice?

No. You tick the boxes.
