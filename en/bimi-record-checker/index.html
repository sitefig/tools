BIMI Record Checker (default._bimi TXT)

Checks a BIMI TXT record for the required v=BIMI1 tag, an https logo link to an SVG file and an optional evidence link. A second box checks your DMARC record against what BIMI asks for.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

BIMI lets mailbox providers show your logo next to authenticated mail. It fails quietly: a wrong tag order, an http link or a DMARC policy of p=none means no logo, and nobody tells you why.

This page checks the record text and the DMARC policy against the BIMI Internet-Draft. It cannot fetch your SVG or certificate, so it cannot confirm the logo file format or that your certificate is valid. Mailbox providers also add rules of their own.

How to use it

  1. Look up the TXT record at default._bimi.yourdomain with your DNS tool.
  2. Paste it into the first box.
  3. Optionally paste your _dmarc TXT record into the second box.
  4. Fix every PROBLEM line and read the REVIEW lines.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the BIMI record published?

As a TXT record at default._bimi.<your domain>, unless you use another selector.

Which DMARC policy is needed?

Quarantine (with pct=100) or reject. A policy of none does not qualify.

Do I need a VMC or CMC?

Some mailbox providers require one. The a= tag points to it. Check the rules of the providers you care about.

Does the tool check the SVG?

No. It only checks the link. The file must be a valid SVG Tiny PS profile.
