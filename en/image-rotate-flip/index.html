Image Rotate & Flip

Rotates images clockwise by any angle, growing the canvas so nothing is cut off, and can mirror them horizontally or vertically. Output as PNG, JPEG or WebP, with batch support.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

A scanned page comes out sideways, a phone photo is mirrored, or a screenshot is a few degrees crooked. Fixing that in an editor means opening a heavy app for a one-second job.

Enter the angle in degrees, clockwise, and tick the flip options you need. Ninety, 180 and 270 give clean quarter turns. Other angles work too, and the canvas is enlarged to hold the rotated picture, which leaves empty corners that are transparent in PNG and WebP and white in JPEG. All processing happens in your browser using a canvas, so private scans and photos are not uploaded anywhere.

How to use it

  1. Choose one or more image files.
  2. Enter a rotation angle in degrees, clockwise.
  3. Tick Flip horizontal or Flip vertical if needed, and select a format and quality.
  4. Run it and download the rotated images.

Frequently asked questions

Which angles work?

Any number. Positive values turn clockwise, negative values turn anticlockwise. Quarter turns of 90, 180 and 270 degrees are lossless in shape, while other angles resample the pixels slightly.

What fills the corners after a non-right-angle rotation?

The canvas grows to fit the rotated image, and the new corners are empty. They stay transparent in PNG and WebP. In JPEG they become white.

Does flipping change the file size or quality?

The picture is redrawn and encoded again, so lossy formats may change size a little. Choose PNG for a lossless result.

Does it fix photos that are sideways because of EXIF orientation?

Browsers usually apply the EXIF orientation when displaying an image, and the tool saves what the browser draws. If a result still looks wrong, rotate it by hand here.
