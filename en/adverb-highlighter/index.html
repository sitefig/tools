Adverb and Weak Qualifier Highlighter

Mark -ly adverbs and weak words like very, really, just and quite in your draft, with counts and a rate per 100 words.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Many editors advise cutting adverbs and softeners that prop up a weak verb. A very big house is a mansion, and she walked slowly may be she crept.

This tool marks every word ending in -ly (except a list of common non-adverbs) and a list of qualifiers such as very, really, quite and just. It counts them per 100 words so you can compare drafts. It is a word-list check, not grammar: it will miss adverbs without -ly and flag some good ones.

How to use it

  1. Paste your draft.
  2. Choose whether to flag -ly words, qualifiers or both.
  3. Read the marked text and the table.
  4. Edit only where the word weakens the sentence.

Frequently asked questions

Does it find every adverb?

No. Adverbs like often or fast do not end in -ly, and some -ly words are adjectives. It is a list-based check.

Should I remove all of them?

No. Use them when they add meaning. The count is there to prompt a second look.

Is my text sent anywhere?

No. The check runs in your browser.
