EU VAT Rates by Country (Standard, Reduced, Super-Reduced, Parking)

A table of VAT rates for every EU member state, searchable by country.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

VAT rates differ widely, from 17% in Luxembourg to 27% in Hungary. The table is bundled in the page, so it works offline and shows its source and date.

Which goods get a reduced rate is set by each country and is not in this table. The source leaves out regional rates. Check your tax authority before invoicing.

How to use it

  1. Type a country code or name, or leave empty for all.
  2. Choose a sort order.
  3. Read the rates and the source line.

Frequently asked questions

How current is the data?

It was copied from the Your Europe VAT page that showed "last checked 13 July 2026". Rates change, so verify.

Why EL for Greece?

The EU uses EL as the VAT code for Greece.

Are regional rates included?

No, for example the Canary Islands and the Azores are left out.
