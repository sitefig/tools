CSAT Score Calculator (Customer Satisfaction %)

Enter response counts per rating and get the CSAT percentage and the average.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

CSAT is the share of responses that are satisfied. Teams differ on whether satisfied means 4 and 5 or only 5, so the cutoff is a choice here.

Always state the cutoff when reporting CSAT. The bars show the distribution, which often says more than the single number.

How to use it

  1. Enter how many responses gave each rating.
  2. Choose the satisfied cutoff.
  3. Read CSAT and the average.
  4. Compare periods with the same cutoff.

Frequently asked questions

What is the formula?

Satisfied responses divided by all responses, times 100.

Which cutoff is standard?

Both are used; 4 and 5 is common.

What about a 10-point scale?

Map it to 5 groups first.

Is data stored?

No.
