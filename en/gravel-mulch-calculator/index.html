Gravel, Mulch & Soil Calculator (Volume and Weight)

Enter the size of the bed or driveway and the depth to see how much material to buy.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Volume is length times width times depth, converted to cubic yards, cubic metres and cubic feet, with an allowance for waste or compaction (10% by default). Weight uses typical densities per cubic yard for gravel, mulch, topsoil, sand and crushed stone, and the bag count divides by the bag size you enter.

Density varies a lot with moisture and grading, so ask your supplier for the weight of the actual product and round the order up.

No data is sent anywhere: the work happens in your browser, so you can use it offline once the page has loaded.

How to use it

  1. Choose feet/inches or metres/centimetres.
  2. Enter length, width and depth.
  3. Pick the material and waste percentage.
  4. Round up when ordering.

Frequently asked questions

What depth should I use?

Mulch 2 to 3 inches, topsoil 4 to 6, gravel paths 2 to 4, driveways 4 to 6 over a base.

Why add a waste allowance?

Material compacts and some is lost in spreading.

Are the weights exact?

No, they are typical densities.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything is processed in your browser, and the page makes no network requests with your input.
