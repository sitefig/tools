Drum Machine (16-Step Sequencer)

A step sequencer with kick, snare, two hi-hats, clap and tom. Click steps on and off, set the tempo and swing, press play, and download the loop as a WAV file.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Sketching a rhythm should take seconds. The grid shows one bar of sixteenth notes; each button is a step you can switch on. Presets give you a starting point: four on the floor, boom bap and a backbeat with an open hat.

All sounds are synthesised in code, so there are no samples to download and no licensing questions. The exported WAV uses the same sounds, mixed offline. The grid is fully operable with the keyboard, and the pattern is stored in the page address so you can share a link.

How to use it

  1. Pick a preset or press steps to switch them on.
  2. Set the tempo and swing.
  3. Press Play to hear the loop; you can edit while it plays.
  4. Choose the number of bars and press Download WAV.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use the beat in my music?

Yes. The sounds are generated by the page, and the exported audio is yours to use.

What does swing do?

It delays every second sixteenth note a little for a shuffled feel. 0 is straight.

Can I share my pattern?

Yes. The pattern, tempo and swing are saved in the address bar; copy the link.

Can I add my own sounds?

Not in this version. The six built-in voices are fixed.
