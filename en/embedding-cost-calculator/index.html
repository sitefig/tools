Embedding Cost Calculator

Enter the number of documents, their average size, the model and how often you re-embed, and see the indexing cost, monthly query cost and the vector storage you will need.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Embedding a corpus looks cheap until you multiply it out: a hundred thousand chunks, a few hundred tokens each, re-run every time you change the chunking or switch model. Then the vector database needs memory for every dimension of every chunk, plus index overhead. People tend to find out about both numbers after the first bill.

This calculator lets you plan before you commit. Choose a listed model or enter your own price per million tokens, and compare scenarios in seconds. List prices change often, so treat the preset prices as a starting point and check your provider. The sums run in your browser and nothing you enter is stored.

How to use it

  1. Pick an embedding model, or choose Custom price and enter the cost per million tokens.
  2. Enter the number of documents or chunks and their average size in tokens, words or characters.
  3. Set how many times you will embed the corpus, the vector dimensions and your monthly query volume.
  4. Read the indexing cost, query cost and estimated storage in the result.

Frequently asked questions

Are the preset prices current?

They are list prices at the time of writing and providers change them often. Use Custom price with the figure from your provider's pricing page for any decision that matters.

How are words and characters turned into tokens?

Words are multiplied by about 1.33 and characters are divided by about 4. These are rough English averages. Other languages and code usually need more tokens, so measure a sample with a real tokenizer.

How is storage calculated?

Documents times dimensions times 4 bytes gives the raw float32 size, and the tool adds a rough 50 percent for an HNSW index. Real overhead depends on your database and its settings.

Tools often used together

  • Embedding Token & Cost EstimatorEstimate tokens, chunk count, API cost and vector storage for embedding your corpus across popular embedding models. Token counts are estimates (characters / ratio) and the price table is a built-in snapshot: override it with your own price.
  • LLM API Cost CalculatorCompute request and monthly cost from tokens and your own per-million prices (prices change; enter current ones).
  • Token Cost Estimator (your own prices)Exact token count (cl100k_base / o200k_base) multiplied by prices YOU enter per 1M tokens. No prices are built in: copy the current figures from your provider's pricing page. Only OpenAI encodings (cl100k_base, o200k_base) are included. Claude, DeepSeek and Gemini tokenizers are NOT included (their vocabularies are not available offline): use the llm-token-estimator tool for approximate counts of those.
  • Embedding Vector SimilarityCompare embedding vectors you paste (JSON arrays or numbers, one vector per line): cosine similarity, dot product, Euclidean and Manhattan distance, plus a similarity matrix and best matches for several vectors. It does not create embeddings.
  • Text Chunk Splitter (RAG)Split text into overlapping chunks by characters or approximate tokens, preferring paragraph/sentence boundaries.
  • RAG Chunking VisualizerSee how a document splits into retrieval chunks: fixed window, sentence or recursive (paragraph, line, sentence, word) splitting with size and overlap. Chunks are color coded and overlaps highlighted (recursive and sentence modes overlap by whole segments, so overlap can be 0 when segments are large).
