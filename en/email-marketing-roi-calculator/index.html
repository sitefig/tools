Email Campaign Revenue and ROI Estimator

Enter list size, the three rates, order value and cost to see projected revenue, net result and ROI.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

The chain from list to order has four steps and each loses most people. Writing it out shows what a change in one rate is worth: lifting clicks by a point may matter more than a long subject line debate.

The result is only as good as the rates you enter, so use figures from your own past sends. Add a margin to get profit instead of revenue. It does not model repeat purchases.

How to use it

  1. Enter the delivered list size.
  2. Enter open rate, click rate of opens and conversion rate of clicks.
  3. Add order value, optional margin and the cost.
  4. Read revenue, net result and ROI.

Frequently asked questions

Is click rate based on opens or sends?

Here it is a share of opens (click-to-open). Convert if your tool reports clicks per delivered email.

Should I add staff time to cost?

Yes, otherwise ROI looks better than it is.

Does it include lifetime value?

No, only the first order.

Is it a forecast?

No, it is a calculation from your assumptions.
