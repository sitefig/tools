Child Adult Height Predictor (Mid-Parental Height)

Estimate adult height from the parents' heights.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Enter the mother's and father's heights and the child's sex. The mid-parental method adds 13 cm to the sum for boys or subtracts 13 cm for girls, divides by two, and shows a range of 8.5 cm either side (about two standard deviations, per the MSD Manual).

It is a population estimate. A child's own growth pattern, nutrition and health matter more, and a doctor can judge a growth chart far better than this formula.

How to use it

  1. Choose cm or inches.
  2. Pick the child's sex.
  3. Enter both parents' heights.
  4. Read the predicted height and range.

Frequently asked questions

How reliable is it?

Most children land within the range, but some do not. It cannot predict an individual.

Does it work for children who are not biological children of both parents?

No. It relies on the parents' genes.

Is this medical advice?

No. It is an estimate from a published formula. A doctor or dietitian can judge your own case far better.

Is anything uploaded?

No. The work happens in your browser and the page makes no network request with your input.
