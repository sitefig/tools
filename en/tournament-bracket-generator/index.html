Tournament Bracket Generator (Single Elimination and Round Robin)

Set up a knockout bracket or a league schedule from your list of teams.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

For single elimination the bracket is padded to the next power of two, seeds are placed in the standard order (1 plays the lowest seed, 2 the second lowest, and so on) and byes go to the top seeds. Round robin uses the circle method so every pair meets exactly once and nobody plays twice in a round; with an odd number one player rests each round. You can shuffle the seeds first. Properties were verified with automated checks.

The output is a list of matches and a printable page; double elimination and result tracking are not included.

No data is sent anywhere: the work happens in your browser, so you can use it offline once the page has loaded.

How to use it

  1. Enter the teams in seed order, one per line.
  2. Choose single elimination or round robin.
  3. Optionally shuffle the seeds.
  4. Print the page or copy the matches.

Frequently asked questions

How are byes assigned?

To the highest seeds, so they skip the first round.

How many matches are there?

Single elimination has players minus one; round robin has n(n-1)/2.

Does it support double elimination?

Not yet.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything is processed in your browser, and the page makes no network requests with your input.
