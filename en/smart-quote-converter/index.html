Smart Quote Converter (Straight to Curly and Back)

Convert straight quotes to typographic curly quotes or the other way round, and optionally dashes and ellipses too.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Word processors turn quotes curly, code and plain text need them straight, and pasting between the two leaves a mix. Curly quotes in source code or JSON break parsers.

Choose the direction. Going curly, a quote after a space, bracket or dash opens and any other closes, which is right for nearly all English prose. Going straight, all curly single and double quotes become ASCII. Leading apostrophes as in '90s are the known weak spot and may need a manual fix.

How to use it

  1. Paste your text.
  2. Choose straight to curly or curly to straight.
  3. Decide whether to convert dashes and ellipses too.
  4. Copy the result and check any '90s style words.

Frequently asked questions

Why is '90s wrong?

A quote after a space looks like an opening quote. The right mark is an apostrophe, which points the other way. Fix those by hand.

Does it fix quotes in code?

It converts every quote it sees, so run it on prose only.

What about other languages?

It produces English style quotes. German, French and other styles use different marks.
