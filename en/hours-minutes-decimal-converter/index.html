Hours and Minutes to Decimal Hours Converter for Billing

Converts hours and minutes (2:45 or 2h 45m) to decimal hours and decimal hours to hours and minutes, with a total and optional rounding up.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Timesheets and invoices need decimal hours: 2 hours 45 minutes is 2.75, not 2.45. The mistake is common.

List several times to get a total. Set a round-up step such as 6 or 15 minutes if you bill in increments.

How to use it

  1. Enter times, one per line.
  2. Optionally set a round-up step in minutes.
  3. Read the conversions and total.

Frequently asked questions

How is the direction chosen?

Lines with a colon or h and m become decimals; plain numbers become h:mm.

Does rounding go up?

Yes, always up to the next step, as most billing does.

Can I use negative times?

Yes, with a minus sign.
