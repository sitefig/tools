DNSSEC DS, DNSKEY and RRSIG Decoder

Decodes DNSSEC records, computes key tags and checks that a DS digest matches the DNSKEY you pasted.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

DNSSEC links a domain to its parent through a DS record, which holds a digest of the domain's key-signing key. If the DS and DNSKEY do not match, validating resolvers reject the domain.

Paste the DS and DNSKEY records from dig and the tool names the algorithms, tells key-signing keys from zone-signing keys, computes the key tag (RFC 4034 appendix B) and compares the digest. It was tested on a live pair of records. It does not verify signatures or walk the chain from the root.

How to use it

  1. Run dig DS and dig DNSKEY for the domain.
  2. Paste the records, plus RRSIG lines if you want date checks.
  3. Read the key tags and the digest match line.
  4. Fix the DS at your registrar if it does not match.

Frequently asked questions

Does it validate DNSSEC?

No. It decodes and compares text. Use a validating resolver for the full chain.

Which digests are checked?

SHA-1, SHA-256 and SHA-384.

Why is my DS not matched?

The DNSKEY with that tag and algorithm is not in your paste, or the DS is out of date.
