Out-of-Office Message Generator (Email Auto-Reply)

Enter your dates and backup contact and get an auto-reply with subject and body.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

A clear out-of-office message states when you are away, when you are back and who to contact. Writing it takes longer than it should, especially with dates spelled out.

Dates are shown with the weekday so there is no doubt. Pick whether you have access to email. Paste the result into your mail client's automatic replies setting.

How to use it

  1. Enter your name and the away and return dates.
  2. Add a backup contact.
  3. Choose a tone and an email access line.
  4. Copy the subject and body.

Frequently asked questions

Should I give a phone number?

Only if the backup contact agrees. It is optional here.

Can I use other languages?

The text is English only.

Do I need a subject?

Yes, auto-replies work better with a clear subject.

Is anything stored?

No.
