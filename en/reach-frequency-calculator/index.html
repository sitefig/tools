Reach, Average Frequency and GRP Calculator

Enter impressions, unique reach and population to get average frequency, reach % and GRPs.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Average frequency is impressions per person reached, reach is the share of the population seen, and GRPs are impressions over population times 100, which equals reach % times frequency.

Frequency is an average, so some people see the ad once and a few see it many times. Reach from different platforms cannot be added because of overlap.

How to use it

  1. Enter total impressions and unique reach.
  2. Enter the target population.
  3. Add spend for cost per GRP and CPM.
  4. Read the results.

Frequently asked questions

What is a GRP?

Gross rating point: one percent of the target population reached, counting repeats.

Can GRPs exceed 100?

Yes, with repeated exposure.

Why check overlap?

The same person may appear on several platforms.

Is data stored?

No.
