Blog Headline & Title Idea Generator (Templates)

Type a topic and get headline starters grouped by type, each with its character count so you can see what may be cut off in search results.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

The generator fills tested title patterns (how-to, numbered lists, questions, guides, comparisons and hooks) with your topic, a number, a year, an adjective and a pain point. Each result shows its length; search results usually show about 50 to 60 characters.

These are templates, not an AI model, so the output is a starting point. Pick the pattern that fits the page, then rewrite the headline in your own words and make sure the page delivers what it promises.

No data is sent anywhere: the work happens in your browser, so you can use it offline once the page has loaded.

How to use it

  1. Enter the topic as a verb phrase, such as "choose running shoes".
  2. Set the number, year, adjective and pain point.
  3. Scan the groups and copy the ones you like.
  4. Rewrite the best one for the specific page.

Frequently asked questions

Are these written by AI?

No. They are fill-in-the-blank templates, which makes the output predictable and private.

How long should a title be?

Aim for under about 60 characters so it is not truncated in search results.

Should I put the keyword first?

Put it early when it reads naturally; clarity beats stuffing.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything is processed in your browser, and the page makes no network requests with your input.
