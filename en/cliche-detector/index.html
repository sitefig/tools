Cliché and Overused Phrase Detector

Mark clichés and worn-out phrases in your text using a built-in list of about 80, with counts.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Clichés are phrases so familiar that readers skim them. They also make writing sound the same as everyone else's.

This tool checks your text against a hand-picked list of about 80 common English clichés and business phrases, marks each hit and counts them. You can add your own phrases. The list is not complete, and idioms can be right in dialogue or humour, so treat each mark as a question.

How to use it

  1. Paste your draft.
  2. Add your own phrases to flag if you like.
  3. Read the marked text and counts.
  4. Rewrite the phrase plainly or freshly.

Frequently asked questions

Is the list complete?

No. It is a short, hand-picked list. Add your own phrases in the option box.

Are clichés always bad?

Not in speech, dialogue or when you use them on purpose. They are a risk in formal prose.

Does it work in other languages?

No, the list is English.
