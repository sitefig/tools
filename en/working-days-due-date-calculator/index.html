Due Date by Working Days Calculator with Your Own Holidays

Moves forward or back a number of working days, skipping weekend days and the holiday dates you paste.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Delivery and legal deadlines count working days. Choose your weekend, add your holidays and get the date.

The start day is not counted. Holidays are only those you list, since the tool does not fetch any calendar.

How to use it

  1. Enter a start date and number of working days.
  2. Pick your weekend days.
  3. List holidays.

Frequently asked questions

Can I subtract days?

Yes, use a negative number.

Does it know public holidays?

No. Paste the dates for your country.

Is the start day counted?

No, counting begins the next working day.
