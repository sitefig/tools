Volume Converter with US and Imperial Gallons

Converts between litres, millilitres, cubic metres and US or UK (imperial) cups, fluid ounces, pints, quarts and gallons.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

A US gallon and a UK gallon are different sizes, 3.785 L against 4.546 L, and a UK pint holds 20 fluid ounces where a US pint holds 16. Mixing them up gives a 20 percent error in a recipe, a fuel figure or a pool.

This tool keeps the two systems in separate rows and uses the exact definitions, so you can see each equivalent at once. The general unit converter has only the US versions.

How to use it

  1. Enter the amount.
  2. Pick the unit you start with.
  3. Read all other units in the list.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a US and a UK gallon?

The US gallon is 231 cubic inches or 3.785 L. The UK imperial gallon is 4.54609 L, about 20 percent larger.

Is a US cup 240 mL?

Here a US cup is 236.588 mL (one sixteenth of a gallon). Nutrition labels round it to 240 mL.

Why are my results long decimals?

Factors are exact, so results show up to ten significant digits. Round them for daily use.
