Ohm's Law Calculator: Volts, Amps, Ohms and Watts

Solves Ohm's law and the power formulas from any two known values among V, I, R and P.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Pick any two values of the four and the rest follow from V = I x R and P = V x I. It is handy for choosing a resistor or checking an LED circuit.

Results use engineering prefixes. It treats the circuit as an ideal resistor, so it does not model reactance or heating.

How to use it

  1. Fill exactly two fields.
  2. Use suffixes like 4.7k or 20m if useful.
  3. Read the other two values.

Frequently asked questions

Why exactly two fields?

Two values fix the rest. One is too few, three could conflict.

Does it work for AC?

For RMS values with a purely resistive load, yes.

What does m mean?

Milli (0.001). Use M for mega.
