Thumbnail Batch Generator (Uniform Square Thumbnails in a ZIP)

Make same-size thumbnails from many images in a ZIP.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Set a width and height, choose centre crop or padding, and drop in your images. Each one becomes a thumbnail of exactly that size. Crop fills the frame and cuts the edges. Padding keeps the whole picture and fills the gaps with a colour.

Results come back in one ZIP. Thumbnails are re-encoded, so EXIF data is not kept.

How to use it

  1. Set the thumbnail width and height.
  2. Choose centre crop or pad.
  3. Pick the format and quality.
  4. Choose the images and download the ZIP.

Frequently asked questions

Crop or pad?

Crop gives a clean grid but cuts the edges. Pad shows everything.

Can I make non-square thumbnails?

Yes. Set any width and height.

Are the images enlarged?

Small images are scaled up to fill the frame, which can look soft.

Is anything uploaded?

No. The work happens in your browser and the page makes no network request with your input.
