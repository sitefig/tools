GDPR Compliance Checklist

An interactive GDPR self-assessment checklist with a progress bar. Your ticks are saved in your own browser and nowhere else. It is a self-check, not legal advice.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

GDPR work is easy to postpone because it is spread across so many small tasks: knowing what data you hold, having a lawful basis, writing notices, handling requests, contracts with processors, breach plans. A checklist turns that into a list you can work through and revisit.

This page groups the common points into sections, shows how many are done and keeps a running percentage. Ticks are stored in your browser's localStorage, so you can close the tab and come back on the same device and browser. Nothing is sent to a server. It is a self-assessment aid to find gaps and not a compliance certificate or legal advice, so have a professional review anything high-risk, such as health data or large-scale monitoring.

How to use it

  1. Open the checklist and read through each section.
  2. Tick the items that you have completed.
  3. Watch the progress bar to see how much is left.
  4. Come back later on the same browser to continue, or use Reset to start again.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved?

In your browser localStorage, on this device only. It is not synced, uploaded or shared. Clearing site data, using private mode or switching browser will lose it.

Does a full score mean I am compliant?

No. The list is a general self-check and cannot know your situation. Compliance needs judgement about your data, risks and processes, ideally with professional advice.

Does it cover laws other than GDPR?

No. It is focused on the GDPR. Other laws such as CCPA or national rules may add requirements that are not listed here.

Can I share the checklist with my team?

Not directly, since progress is stored locally. Each person has their own copy. For shared tracking, copy the items into a document or task tool.
