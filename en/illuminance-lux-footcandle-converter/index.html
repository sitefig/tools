Illuminance Converter: Lux and Foot-Candles

Converts illuminance between lux, foot-candles and phot and lists rough light levels from moonlight to direct sun.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Light meters and lighting guides use lux in most of the world and foot-candles in the US. One foot-candle is 10.76 lux.

A short list of typical levels helps you judge a reading. They are rough orientation values, not a lighting standard.

How to use it

  1. Enter the light level.
  2. Choose lux, foot-candle or phot.
  3. Compare the result with the typical levels.

Frequently asked questions

How many lux is 50 foot-candles?

About 538 lux.

What lux is right for an office?

Guides often say 300 to 500 lux for desk work. Check your local standard.

Is lux the same as lumens?

No. Lux is lumens per square metre, so it depends on area.
