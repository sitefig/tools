Plain Language Checker: Complex Words, Long Sentences and Passive Voice

Flags complex words, long sentences and passive phrases in English text.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Plain language helps readers with low literacy, cognitive disabilities, or a second language.

The tool uses a list of about 120 word pairs, a sentence length limit and a rough passive pattern. It shows a version with only the word swaps, which may need grammar fixes. It does not understand meaning and is for English only.

How to use it

  1. Paste your text.
  2. Set the long sentence limit.
  3. Review the word swaps and sentences.
  4. Rewrite in your own words.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a readability score?

No. Use the Readability Scores tool for that.

Why can the swaps be wrong?

Word-for-word replacement ignores grammar and context.

Does it cover legal terms?

Keep terms your readers need.

Is my text uploaded?

No.
