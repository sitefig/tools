Work Hours & Time Card Calculator (Overtime, Pay)

Paste a week of start and end times and get totals, overtime and pay.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Enter shifts like "Mon 09:00 - 17:30 30" (label optional, break in minutes at the end). Shifts that end before they start count as overnight. You can set daily overtime after a number of hours, weekly overtime after a threshold (40 by default), the hourly rate and the overtime multiplier.

Overtime rules vary by country, contract and state (daily, weekly, double time, rest-day premiums), so set the thresholds your rules use and check payroll law for official calculations.

No data is sent anywhere: the work happens in your browser, so you can use it offline once the page has loaded.

How to use it

  1. List each shift on its own line.
  2. Set overtime thresholds, rate and multiplier.
  3. Read the total, regular and overtime hours.
  4. Copy the figures into your timesheet.

Frequently asked questions

How are overnight shifts handled?

If the end time is earlier than the start, the shift is counted as ending the next day.

Can I use 12-hour times?

Enter 24-hour times; use the time format converter first for AM/PM.

Is this payroll software?

No, it is a calculator. Taxes and statutory rules are not applied.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything is processed in your browser, and the page makes no network requests with your input.
