Branded vs Non-Branded Query Splitter

Separate searches that include your brand from generic ones, and compare how many clicks and impressions each group brings.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Paste queries one per line or as CSV with clicks and impressions. A query counts as branded if it contains any brand term, ignoring spaces and dashes (so "acmeshoes" matches "acme shoes"). An option also catches one-letter typos of brand words of five or more letters.

Branded traffic usually has a very high CTR and says little about SEO growth, so tracking non-branded clicks separately is the useful number. Add product names and common misspellings to the brand list for a cleaner split.

No data is sent anywhere: the work happens in your browser, so you can use it offline once the page has loaded.

How to use it

  1. Export queries from Search Console (with clicks and impressions) and paste them.
  2. Enter your brand terms separated by commas.
  3. Run it and compare branded and non-branded totals and CTR.
  4. Refine the brand list if queries land in the wrong group.

Frequently asked questions

Does it use Google's own branded filter?

No. Search Console has a similar built-in filter; this tool lets you control the terms and works with any exported list.

What does the typo option do?

It treats a word that is one edit away from a brand term (5+ letters) as branded, for example "akme" for "acme".

Why is branded CTR so high?

People searching for you by name usually want your site and click the first result.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything is processed in your browser, and the page makes no network requests with your input.
