Affiliate Commission Calculator (Tiered or Flat)

Define tiers and enter sales to see the payout per tier and in total.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Tiered plans are easy to misread: either each slice earns its own rate (marginal) or the whole amount earns the rate of the tier reached (retroactive). The difference can be large.

Choose the mode your program uses. Tier ranges must not overlap. Check your program terms for refunds and whether tiers use gross or net sales.

How to use it

  1. Enter tiers as from - to : rate%.
  2. Enter the sales amount.
  3. Choose marginal or retroactive.
  4. Read the payout and effective rate.

Frequently asked questions

Which mode is common?

Both exist. Read your terms.

Can I use a flat rate?

Yes, a single tier from 0 with no upper limit.

Does it handle refunds?

No.

Is data stored?

No.
