Ringtone Maker (Cut a Clip up to 30 Seconds)

Cuts a short clip from an audio file, adds a fade at each end, optionally raises it to a standard peak level and saves a WAV file you can convert for your phone.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Most phones limit ringtones to about 30 seconds, and a clip that starts and ends abruptly sounds unpleasant. A short fade-in avoids a click when the ringtone starts and a fade-out avoids a cut-off when it loops.

The browser has no encoder for MP3 or iPhone M4R files, so this tool outputs WAV. Convert it with your phone or a converter afterwards. Android generally accepts MP3, OGG and often WAV; iPhone uses M4R of at most 30 seconds.

How to use it

  1. Choose a song.
  2. Enter the start in seconds and the length (up to 30).
  3. Set the fades and decide whether to normalise the peak.
  4. Press Run, check the result, and download the WAV.

Frequently asked questions

Why not M4R or MP3 directly?

Browsers do not provide encoders for them and this site does not ship third-party code.

How do I find the start time?

Play the song in any player and note the time of the part you want, then type it in seconds.

What does normalising the peak do?

It raises the clip until its loudest point is at -1 dBFS, so quiet songs ring clearly.

Can I use a longer clip?

Use the audio trimmer for any length. This tool caps the length at 30 seconds.
