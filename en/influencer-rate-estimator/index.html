Influencer Sponsored Post Rate Estimator (Editable Rate Card)

Estimate a fair price for a sponsored post from followers and engagement, using a rate per 1,000 followers that you set yourself.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Creators and brands often ask what a post is worth. No single market rate exists, and public rate tables vary by platform, niche and year.

This tool is a calculator for your own rate card. Set a base rate per 1,000 followers, compare your engagement rate with an average you choose, add a format multiplier and a usage-rights add-on, and see an estimate with a rough negotiation band. The defaults are placeholders, not data.

How to use it

  1. Enter followers and engagement rate.
  2. Set your base rate per 1,000 followers and the average engagement rate.
  3. Add a format multiplier and usage rights if needed.
  4. Use the estimate as a starting point for a quote.

Frequently asked questions

Where do the default numbers come from?

They are placeholders. Replace them with rates you have been paid or quoted.

How is engagement used?

Your rate divided by the average, kept between 0.5 and 2 times.

Is the band exact?

No. It is plus or minus 25 percent as a negotiation range.
