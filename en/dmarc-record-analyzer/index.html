DMARC Record Analyzer (Explain a TXT Value)

Explains each DMARC tag in a pasted record and warns about weak or invalid settings.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

A DMARC record controls what receivers do with mail that fails SPF and DKIM alignment, and where they send reports. The syntax is short, but a typo can disable the policy.

Paste the record (quotes and split strings are joined) and see the policy, subdomain policy, percentage, alignment modes and report addresses in plain words. Warnings cover p=none, pct below 100, missing rua, bad URIs and unknown tags. It reads RFC 7489 tags and accepts newer ones without checking. It does not look up DNS.

How to use it

  1. Run dig TXT _dmarc.yourdomain or copy the value from DNS.
  2. Paste it.
  3. Read the explanation and the review list.
  4. Move from none to quarantine to reject as reports allow.

Frequently asked questions

What does p=none do?

It only monitors. Nothing is blocked.

Why do external report addresses need a record?

The receiving domain must authorise reports for yours, otherwise they are not sent.

Can it write a record?

Use the DMARC Record Generator for that.

Tools often used together

  • DMARC Record GeneratorBuild the _dmarc TXT record: policy, subdomain policy, percentage, aggregate/forensic reports, alignment modes.
  • DMARC Aggregate Report (RUA XML) ViewerOpen DMARC aggregate report XML (also .gz or .zip) and see pass and fail counts per sending IP, the policy applied, and which sources fail both SPF and DKIM alignment.
  • SPF Record GeneratorCompose an SPF TXT record from mechanisms, count DNS lookups against the 10-lookup limit and check the length.

  • dig and nslookup Output Analyzer (MX, NS, TXT, SPF, DMARC, DKIM)Paste the output of dig or nslookup and get the records grouped and checked: SPF lookup count and policy, DMARC policy, DKIM key, MX order, NS count, CNAME clashes, TTLs, DNSSEC and error status.
  • Email Header Analyzer (Received Path, SPF, DKIM, DMARC)Paste full email headers to trace the route (each Received hop with delay), read SPF, DKIM and DMARC results, check From / Return-Path alignment and spot spoofing signs.
