Minesweeper

Play beginner-level Minesweeper: a 9 by 9 board with 10 hidden mines. Reveal safe squares, use the numbers to find mines, and flag them to win.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Minesweeper is a small logic puzzle: every number tells you how many mines touch that square, and from those clues you work out which squares are safe. A round takes a minute or two, which suits a short break.

This version uses the beginner board. Your first click is always safe and never next to a mine, so you do not lose on move one. Right-click to place a flag on a computer, or tick "Flag mode" and tap on a touch screen. A counter shows mines left and a timer starts at your first click. Your best time is kept in this browser only. There is no account and no advertising.

How to use it

  1. Click a square to reveal it. Your first click is always safe.
  2. Read the numbers: each shows how many of the eight neighbouring squares contain a mine.
  3. Right-click to flag a suspected mine, or tick "Flag mode" and click or tap squares to flag them.
  4. Reveal every safe square to win. Press "New game" to play again and beat your best time.

Frequently asked questions

Is the first click always safe?

Yes. Mines are placed after your first click, avoiding that square and its neighbours, so the first click opens up an area rather than ending the game.

How do I flag on a phone or tablet?

Tick the "Flag mode" checkbox below the board. Taps then place or remove flags instead of revealing squares. Untick it to reveal squares again.

Where is my best time stored?

In your browser's local storage on this device. It is not uploaded, and clearing site data or using a private window removes it.

Can I change the board size or number of mines?

No. The board is fixed at 9 by 9 with 10 mines, the usual beginner level, so every round is directly comparable with your saved best time.
