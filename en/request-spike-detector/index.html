Request Rate Spike Detector: Minutes with Unusual Traffic or Errors

Counts requests and errors per bucket and flags outliers against a median and MAD baseline.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

A single busy minute can be a crawler, a deploy or an attack. The tool builds a per-bucket series and marks buckets far above the typical level.

The baseline is the median plus a multiple of the robust spread (1.4826 times the median absolute deviation). It is a simple statistic, not a forecast, and flagged buckets are outliers, not proof of a problem.

How to use it

  1. Paste an access log or any timestamped log.
  2. Choose the bucket size and sensitivity.
  3. Set a minimum count to ignore tiny buckets.
  4. Read the spikes and the bar chart.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as an error?

5xx status in access logs, or an ERROR or FATAL level in other logs.

Why use the median?

A spike does not move the median, so it does not hide itself.

What about a quiet site?

With a median near zero any busy bucket above the minimum is flagged.

Is my log uploaded?

No. Everything is processed in your browser and nothing is sent. Logs can hold IP addresses and tokens, so the page does not write them into the address bar.
