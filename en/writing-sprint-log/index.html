Writing Sprint Log: Words per Minute from Timed Sprints

Log your timed writing sprints (minutes and words) and see words per minute for each, your best sprint and a pace for a daily target.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Timed sprints make a writing session measurable. Seeing words per minute shows which conditions work for you.

Enter one line per sprint: minutes and words, with an optional note. The tool shows pace for each sprint, the average and best, and how many more sprints of your chosen length reach a daily target at your average pace. For the timer itself use the Pomodoro Timer on this site.

How to use it

  1. Write one line per sprint, for example: 25 480 chapter 3.
  2. Set your target for the day and sprint length.
  3. Read the table and the pace.
  4. Plan the next sprint.

Frequently asked questions

Does it time my sprint?

No. Use the Pomodoro Timer, then log the result here.

Is the log saved?

No. Keep it in your notes and paste it back.

What is a good pace?

It depends on the person and the task. Compare yourself with your own earlier sprints.
