Dimensional (Volumetric) Weight Calculator With Carrier Divisors

Computes volumetric weight from length, width, height and a divisor, and shows which weight a carrier would bill.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Carriers bill the greater of actual weight and volumetric weight, so a light, bulky box can cost as much as a heavy one. The formula is length times width times height divided by a divisor, and the divisor differs by carrier and service.

The divisors offered here, about 5000, 6000 and 7000 cm3 per kg, come from a public summary of carrier practice that is not dated per carrier. Confirm the one your carrier uses and how it rounds, then use Custom if it differs.

How to use it

  1. Enter the box size and the actual weight.
  2. Choose a divisor or enter your carrier's.
  3. Read the volumetric weight and the billable weight.

Frequently asked questions

Which divisor does my carrier use?

Ask your carrier or read its current rate guide. Values around 5000, 6000 and 7000 are common, but they change.

Do carriers round?

Many round dimensions or weight up. This tool does not, so allow some margin.

Does it use inches and pounds?

Yes. Pick the units and the tool converts.
