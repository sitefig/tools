3D Model Viewer (STL, OBJ, PLY, GLB, 3MF) with Volume and Size

Check a 3D model before printing or sharing, without installing software.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

The viewer parses binary and ASCII STL, OBJ (triangulating polygons, supporting negative indices), PLY (ASCII and binary), glTF/GLB (triangle meshes with node transforms, self-contained files) and 3MF (meshes, components and build transforms), centres the model and draws it with a simple software renderer using flat shading. Controls are range sliders and buttons so it works with a keyboard. It reports triangle count, bounding box, surface area and volume computed with signed tetrahedra; volume is meaningful only for closed meshes. Parsing and measurements were verified on a unit cube in all formats.

The preview is a basic painter's-algorithm rendering of up to 60,000 triangles, not a full 3D engine; the numbers use every triangle. FBX and glTF files that reference external buffers are not supported, and materials and textures are ignored.

No data is sent anywhere: the work happens in your browser, so you can use it offline once the page has loaded.

How to use it

  1. Choose a .stl, .obj, .ply, .glb or .gltf file, or load the sample cube.
  2. Use Turn, Tilt, Zoom or the buttons.
  3. Read size, area and volume.
  4. Fix non-manifold meshes in your CAD tool before printing.

Frequently asked questions

What units are shown?

Whatever the file uses; STL has no units. Millimetres are typical, so cm3 is shown for convenience.

Why is the volume odd?

Open or self-intersecting meshes give meaningless volumes.

Can it handle large files?

The preview samples triangles above 60,000, but very large files can still be slow.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything is processed in your browser, and the page makes no network requests with your input.
