Question Keyword Extractor: Filter a Keyword List to Questions

Keeps keywords that start with a question word (or end with a question mark), and groups them by that word.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Question keywords tell you what people want to know, and they fit FAQ sections, how-to guides and featured snippet answers. In a list of thousands they are hard to spot.

The filter looks for English question words at the start of a keyword, and optionally anywhere in it. It does not understand meaning: a keyword that starts with "can" or "is" may be a product name.

How to use it

  1. Paste your keyword list.
  2. Decide whether to include keywords with question words later in the phrase.
  3. Read the groups.
  4. Turn the best questions into headings with short direct answers.

Frequently asked questions

Which words count as question words?

who, whom, whose, what, when, where, why, how, which, can, could, do, does, did, is, are, was, were, will, would, should, shall, may, might, has, have, am.

Does it handle other languages?

No, English only.

Why are some questions missing?

Unusual phrasing such as "best way to X" is not a question word start unless you tick the "anywhere" option.

What next?

Group them with the keyword clusterer or check intent with the intent classifier.
