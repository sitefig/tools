Document Scanner: Straighten, Clean Up and Save Photos of Pages as PDF

Load a photo of a page, find its corners automatically or type them, straighten the perspective, clean up the lighting and collect pages into one PDF. Nothing is uploaded.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

A phone photo of a page has a tilted shape, a shadow across one side and a grey background. This tool finds the page as the biggest light or dark area, takes its four extreme points as corners, warps the page into a rectangle and divides out the slowly changing light so the paper turns white. Black and white mode uses a local threshold, which keeps faint text that a single global threshold would lose.

The corner finder is a simple heuristic, not a trained model: it works best on a page on a plain, contrasting surface, and you can always set the corners by hand, with the pointer, with [ ] and the arrow keys, or with number boxes. A photo taken at an angle cannot tell the true page proportions, so you can pick A4 or Letter. Pages go into a PDF written by the page itself: colour pages as JPEG, grey and black and white pages as compressed greyscale.

How to use it

  1. Choose a photo of a page (on a phone, the file dialog offers the camera).
  2. Press "Find the page corners automatically", then check them and fix any by hand.
  3. Choose the clean-up mode and page shape, press "Straighten and clean up", then "Add this page to the PDF".
  4. Repeat for more pages, reorder them, and download the PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Does it use the camera directly?

No. You choose a picture file; on most phones the file dialog lets you take a new photo. Live camera capture is not included.

Why does the page come out the wrong proportions?

A photo shot at an angle does not say how long the page really is. Pick A4 or Letter in Page shape, or leave it on the measured shape.

How accurate is the corner detection?

On a clean test photo it landed within a few percent of the picture size. Cluttered backgrounds, curled pages or a page that blends with the table can fool it, so always check the corners.

Is there OCR?

No. The PDF contains images only. The site has a separate OCR tool for extracting text.
