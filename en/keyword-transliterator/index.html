Transliteration Keyword Tool: Cyrillic, Greek and Japanese Kana to Latin

Converts keywords written in Cyrillic (Russian or Ukrainian), Greek or Japanese hiragana and katakana into Latin letters, with an optional URL slug.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

People often type foreign-language queries with Latin letters, and URL slugs in Latin letters are easier to share. A rule-based romanization gives you a quick first version of both.

These are simplified rules, not official standards, and I did not check them against the standard texts, so do not use them for passports or legal names. Kanji and Chinese characters cannot be converted without a dictionary and are left unchanged. Chinese pinyin is not supported. Always check real search queries in Search Console.

How to use it

  1. Paste your keywords, one per line.
  2. Choose the script.
  3. Tick the slug option if you need URL forms.
  4. Check the result with a native speaker for important pages.

Frequently asked questions

Why not pinyin?

Pinyin needs a dictionary of thousands of characters, with several readings per character. It is not included.

Which standard is used?

None strictly. Russian is close to BGN/PCGN, Ukrainian follows the common national scheme with initial and medial forms, Japanese follows Hepburn without macrons.

Are accents in Greek removed?

Yes, accents are stripped before converting.

Should URLs be in Latin letters?

Google handles non-Latin URLs, but Latin slugs are easier to share and to type. Choose by audience.
