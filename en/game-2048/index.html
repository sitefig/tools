2048

Play the classic 2048 puzzle on a 4 by 4 grid. Slide tiles with the arrow keys, a swipe or the on-screen buttons, merge equal numbers and aim for the highest score.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Sometimes the most useful thing on a tools page is a two-minute break. 2048 is a good one: the rules fit in a sentence, a round is quick, and it still rewards a little planning, such as keeping your largest tile in a corner.

This version runs entirely in your browser, with no account, no adverts and no download. Keyboard arrows work on a computer, and swipes work on a touch screen, with buttons as a fallback. Your best score is kept in this browser only. The game follows the usual rules, with new tiles of 2 or 4 after each move, and ends when no move is left.

How to use it

  1. Press the arrow keys, swipe on the board or use the Left, Up, Down and Right buttons to slide all tiles.
  2. When two tiles with the same number collide, they merge into one with the sum.
  3. Watch the score and best score at the top, and plan moves so the board does not fill up.
  4. Press "New game" to start again at any time.

Frequently asked questions

Is my best score saved?

Yes, in your browser's local storage on this device. It is not uploaded and will not follow you to another device. Clearing site data or using a private window resets it.

What are the rules?

Each move slides every tile as far as it can go. Tiles with equal values merge once per move, and a new 2 or 4 appears afterwards. The game ends when the grid is full and no merge is possible.

Why do the arrow keys not scroll the page?

While playing, the arrow keys are captured by the game. Typing in a text field elsewhere on the page still works normally.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Swipe on the board, or tap the buttons beneath it. Swipes shorter than a few pixels are ignored to avoid accidental moves.
