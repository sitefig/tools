Weighted Grade Calculator (and Final Exam Grade Needed)

Find out where your grade stands and what the final exam has to be.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Enter lines like "Midterm 84 30" (name, score, weight %). The page computes the weighted grade on the work graded so far, the points already banked toward the final grade, and the average you need on the remaining weight to reach your target, with best and worst case outcomes. Letter grades use a US scale or a plus/minus scale.

Grading policies differ (curves, dropped scores, rounding), so check your syllabus for the official calculation.

No data is sent anywhere: the work happens in your browser, so you can use it offline once the page has loaded.

How to use it

  1. List each graded item with score and weight.
  2. Enter your target grade.
  3. Read the current grade and required average.
  4. Check the policy for rounding and drops.

Frequently asked questions

Do weights have to add to 100?

No, but they cannot exceed 100. The rest is treated as remaining work.

What if the needed score is over 100?

The target is out of reach without extra credit.

Can I use percentages and points?

Enter scores as percentages.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything is processed in your browser, and the page makes no network requests with your input.
