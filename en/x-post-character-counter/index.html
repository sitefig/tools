X (Twitter) Post Character Counter with URL Weighting

Count a post the way X does: links count as 23, emoji and CJK count as 2, and the limit is 280 weighted characters.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

X does not count characters one by one. Every link counts 23 whatever its length, and emoji and many non-Latin characters count double. A post that looks short can be over the limit.

This counter applies the published weighting (the twitter-text configuration, version 3: Latin ranges weigh 1, other characters 2, links 23, limit 280) and shows the plain count next to it. You can change the limit for accounts with a longer allowance. The text stays in your browser.

How to use it

  1. Paste your draft.
  2. Read the weighted length and what remains.
  3. Change the limit if your account allows longer posts.
  4. Shorten the text until the remaining number is zero or more.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my link count 23?

X shortens every link and counts it as 23 characters, whatever the real length.

Why do emoji and Japanese count double?

X gives characters outside certain Latin and punctuation ranges a weight of 2, so only 140 of them fit.

Are links without https detected?

Only http(s):// and www. links are detected here. X may also link bare domains, so leave a little room.
