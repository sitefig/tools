Email Campaign Rates Calculator (Open, Click, Bounce, Unsubscribe)

Enter the counts from one send and get every standard email rate, with flags where a rate is above a common limit.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Open rate, CTR and CTOR are often mixed up because each divides by something different. Here each rate states its formula: opens and clicks are divided by delivered emails, CTOR is clicks over opens, and bounce rates use emails sent.

The complaint limits come from Google's email sender guidelines (keep below 0.1%, never reach 0.3%). The 2% bounce and 0.5% unsubscribe flags are common rules of thumb, not rules. Open counts are inflated by Apple Mail Privacy Protection, so compare your own sends over time instead of chasing industry averages.

How to use it

  1. Enter emails sent, bounces, opens, clicks, unsubscribes and complaints.
  2. Leave Delivered at 0 to use sent minus bounces.
  3. Read the rates and the review flags.
  4. Compare with your previous sends.

Frequently asked questions

Should I use unique or total opens?

Unique opens and unique clicks. Total counts include repeat opens by the same person.

What is click-to-open rate?

Unique clicks divided by unique opens. It shows how engaging the content was for people who opened.

Why are my open rates so high?

Mail privacy features load images automatically and count as opens. Clicks are a safer signal.

Where do the limits come from?

Complaint limits are from Google's sender guidelines. The others are rules of thumb.
