Waist-to-Hip Ratio Calculator (WHO Cut-offs)

Compare your waist-to-hip ratio with the WHO cut-offs.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Enter your waist and hip measurements and your sex. The page divides one by the other and compares it with 0.90 for men and 0.85 for women, the cut-offs usually attributed to the WHO 2008 expert consultation as a substantially increased metabolic risk.

The figures come from secondary sources; the WHO report itself was not checked. Cut-offs can differ for some ethnic groups. A result above the line is a reason to talk to a health professional, not a diagnosis.

How to use it

  1. Choose cm or inches and your sex.
  2. Measure the waist and the widest part of the hips.
  3. Enter both numbers.
  4. Read the ratio and the cut-off result.

Frequently asked questions

How do I measure the hips?

Stand relaxed and measure around the widest part of the buttocks, with the tape level.

Which sex do I choose?

The cut-offs were set for men and women separately. Use the one that matches the reference group your doctor uses.

Is this medical advice?

No. It is an estimate from a published formula. A doctor or dietitian can judge your own case far better.

Is anything uploaded?

No. The work happens in your browser and the page makes no network request with your input.
