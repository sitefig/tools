YouTube Title, Description & Tags Checker

Run a quick pre-upload check of the text around a video: title length, description length, hashtags, chapters and tags.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

The checks follow YouTube's published limits: titles up to 100 characters, descriptions up to 5,000, tags up to 500 characters in total, and ignored hashtags when a video has more than 15. It also validates chapter timestamps (first at 00:00, at least three, ascending, each chapter at least 10 seconds) and warns when a link sits in the first 150 characters, which is all that shows before "more".

None of this guarantees reach: click-through rate and watch time matter far more than metadata. Limits can change, so confirm in YouTube's help centre.

No data is sent anywhere: the work happens in your browser, so you can use it offline once the page has loaded.

How to use it

  1. Paste the title, description and tags (comma separated).
  2. Run the checker.
  3. Fix any ERROR or WARN lines.
  4. Upload with a title that matches the thumbnail promise.

Frequently asked questions

How many tags can I use?

Up to 500 characters in total. YouTube says tags matter little beyond helping with misspellings.

What makes chapters work?

A 00:00 start, at least three timestamps in ascending order and at least 10 seconds between them.

Is the title length limit 60?

The hard limit is 100 characters; around 60 is what often shows in results.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything is processed in your browser, and the page makes no network requests with your input.
