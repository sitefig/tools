CSV Coordinates to Map Plotter (Points on a Country-Outline Map)

Reads a CSV with lat and lon columns and draws each row as a marker over country outlines, sized by an optional column.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

A quick look at where your rows are. No street map is loaded, so nothing about your locations leaves the page; the outlines come from a public domain dataset on this site. The view zooms to your points.

Columns are found by name: lat or latitude, lon, lng or longitude. Rows with invalid coordinates are skipped and counted. For Europe-sized spreads the outline is coarse (110m scale), so use it for context, not for fine positions.

How to use it

  1. Paste a CSV with a header.
  2. Name the label and size columns if you want them.
  3. Switch the outlines on or off.
  4. Copy or download the SVG.

Frequently asked questions

Which column names are recognised?

lat, latitude, y and lon, lng, long, longitude, x.

Why no street map?

It would need to contact a tile server.

How precise is it?

The data is exact; the outline is coarse.

Limit?

5000 points.
