Snippet Control Advisor: nosnippet, max-snippet, data-nosnippet

Gives the markup for limiting snippets, with an honest note on what Google says about AI features.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Google's page on AI features lists nosnippet, data-nosnippet, max-snippet and noindex as the controls for limiting what is shown from your pages in Search, and says a page must be indexed and eligible for a snippet to appear as a supporting link.

Pick a goal and get the exact markup. Google does not spell out beyond that how each control changes AI Overviews or AI Mode, so the tool does not claim more. Google-Extended is a separate token for Gemini training and grounding and does not affect Search.

How to use it

  1. Choose your goal.
  2. Set the excerpt length if needed.
  3. Copy the tag, header or attribute.
  4. Check results after recrawling, which can take days.

Frequently asked questions

Does nosnippet remove my page from AI Overviews?

Google lists it among the controls but does not spell out the exact effect.

What is data-nosnippet?

An attribute for span, div or section that keeps that part out of snippets.

Does noindex remove the page from Search?

Yes, once the crawler sees it.

Does Google-Extended affect Search?

No, per Google.
