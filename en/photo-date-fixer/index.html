Photo Date Fixer (Shift EXIF Capture Time in Batch)

Shift the capture time of photos in EXIF.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Choose JPEGs and either shift every date by an offset such as +6h or -1d 2h 30m, or set the earliest photo to a date you give and move the rest by the same amount. DateTime, DateTimeOriginal and DateTimeDigitized are changed in place.

EXIF stores a local clock time without a time zone, so the tool shifts the written time and does not handle time zones or daylight saving. XMP dates, GPS time and the file modified time are not changed.

How to use it

  1. Choose the JPEGs.
  2. Pick shift or set mode.
  3. Enter the offset or the new date.
  4. Download the files.

Frequently asked questions

Does the sign apply to every part?

Yes. -1d 2h means minus one day and two hours.

Why do some files get skipped?

They have no EXIF date tags.

Does it change the file modified time?

No. That belongs to your file system.

Is anything uploaded?

No. The work happens in your browser and the page makes no network request with your input.
