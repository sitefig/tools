Writing Goal Tracker: Daily Word Count Streaks from Your Log

Paste a log of dates and words written, set a daily goal, and see your streak, longest streak, average and the days you hit the goal.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Daily word-count goals work because streaks show. The hard part is keeping the numbers somewhere.

Paste a log with one line per day, set your goal and optionally a project total. The tool counts the current and longest streak, the share of days you hit the goal, averages and a projected finish at your recent pace. Nothing is stored by the page: you keep the log in your own notes and paste it back.

How to use it

  1. Write one line per day: 2026-03-01 620.
  2. Paste the log and set your daily goal.
  3. Optionally set today's date and a project target.
  4. Read the streaks and the last days table.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my log saved?

Nowhere. The page does not store anything, so keep the log in your own file.

How is the current streak counted?

Days in a row that met the goal, ending today if met, or yesterday if today is not over.

What about days with no line?

They count as zero words.
