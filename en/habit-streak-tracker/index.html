Habit Streak Tracker

Add habits, tick them off each day and see your current streak, best streak and total days. A 14-day strip shows recent progress, and data is saved only in this browser.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Habit apps often ask for an account, notifications and a subscription, when all most people want is a checklist that shows how many days in a row they have kept going. This tracker offers exactly that. Add a habit such as reading or stretching, press Done today, and it works out your current streak, your best ever run and the total number of days. A strip of filled and empty squares shows the last two weeks so a missed day is obvious. Everything is saved in your browser's local storage, which means no sign-in and no sync: the data stays on this device and browser, and clearing site data or switching browsers starts afresh.

How to use it

  1. Type a habit name and press 'Add habit'.
  2. Press 'Done today' each day you complete it; press again to undo.
  3. Check the streak, best streak, total and the 14-day strip, or press Delete to remove a habit.

Frequently asked questions

How is the current streak counted?

As consecutive days ending today. If you have not ticked today yet, the streak still counts up to yesterday, so it does not drop to zero until a full day is missed.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser's local storage on this device. It is not uploaded, not synced between devices and not backed up. Clearing site data, or using a private window, will delete it.

Can I mark an earlier day?

No. You can only mark or unmark today. The strip shows the past 14 days, but past days cannot be edited.

What if I use the tracker on another device?

You will start with an empty list there, because nothing syncs. Use one browser consistently for the history to make sense.
