Display Test (Dead Pixels, Colours, Refresh Rate)

Inspect a new monitor or phone for defects.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Choose solid colours (black, white, red, green, blue, grey and secondaries) and use the browser's full screen to cover the whole display, then look for specks that do not match. A black-to-white gradient helps reveal banding, and a two-second timing test estimates the refresh rate from animation frame intervals.

Colours change only when you press a button, so nothing flashes. The refresh figure is what the browser reports; power saving or background tabs can lower it.

No data is sent anywhere: the work happens in your browser, so you can use it offline once the page has loaded.

How to use it

  1. Press a colour button, then Full screen.
  2. Scan the screen for specks.
  3. Check the gradient for visible steps.
  4. Run the refresh rate test.

Frequently asked questions

What is a stuck pixel?

A pixel that stays on a colour. Dead pixels stay black.

Can stuck pixels be fixed?

Sometimes by gentle massage or pixel-exercising videos; warranty policies vary.

Why is my refresh rate 60 Hz?

The browser may limit frame rate; check your OS display settings.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything is processed in your browser, and the page makes no network requests with your input.
