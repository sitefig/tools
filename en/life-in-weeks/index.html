Life in Weeks Calendar

See a lifetime as a grid, one square per week.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Enter a birth date and an expected lifespan. The page counts the full weeks lived, weeks in the chosen lifespan and weeks left, and draws a grid of 52 columns by one row per year with lived weeks filled. The preview can be printed or saved as a PDF.

The lifespan is only a planning number, not a prediction; real life expectancy varies widely with country, health and chance.

No data is sent anywhere: the work happens in your browser, so you can use it offline once the page has loaded.

How to use it

  1. Enter your birth date.
  2. Set the lifespan you want to plan around.
  3. Read the numbers and the chart.
  4. Print it if you like.

Frequently asked questions

Is it accurate?

The week count is exact; the lifespan is whatever you choose.

Is my birth date stored?

No, it stays in the page.

Why 52 columns?

A year has 52 weeks plus one or two days.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything is processed in your browser, and the page makes no network requests with your input.
