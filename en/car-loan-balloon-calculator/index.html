Car Finance Calculator with Deposit and Balloon Payment

Monthly instalment, interest and total cost for a car loan that ends with a balloon payment.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

A balloon payment lowers the monthly instalment because part of the loan is left to the end. That balance still accrues interest all along, so total interest is higher than for a loan paid down fully.

Use the tool to compare a plain loan (balloon 0) with a balloon deal on the same price and rate. Remember you must pay, refinance or return the car at the end, depending on the contract.

How to use it

  1. Enter car price, deposit, rate and term in months.
  2. Enter the balloon amount, or 0 for a normal loan.
  3. Compare the monthly payment and total cost.

Frequently asked questions

Is the balloon included in total cost?

Yes. Total paid to the lender includes all instalments and the balloon.

Are fees included?

No. Arrangement fees, insurance and taxes are not part of the calculation.

What if I cannot pay the balloon?

That depends on the contract: refinance, sell the car or hand it back. Read the terms before signing.
