Vesting Schedule Calculator (Cliff and Monthly Vesting)

Calculates vested shares over time for a schedule with a cliff and monthly vesting.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

A typical grant vests over four years with a one-year cliff: nothing vests until the cliff date, then a share for each month vests. The calculator shows the amount at the cliff and later months, rounding down to whole shares.

Give start date, length, cliff and a check date. Plans differ on rounding, step size and acceleration, so follow your grant document for the final figure.

How to use it

  1. Enter shares, start date, length and cliff.
  2. Enter a check date.
  3. Read the vested amount and the schedule.

Frequently asked questions

What happens at the cliff?

The shares for the cliff period vest at once.

Does it handle acceleration?

No.

How are fractions handled?

Rounded down; the last month vests the rest.
