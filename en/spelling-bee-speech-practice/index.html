Spelling Practice: Hear the Word, Type the Spelling

Reads each word of your list aloud with your browser's speech synthesis; you type it, and at the end you see your score and the words to practise.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Spelling practice works best with spoken words, like a classroom test. Paste your weekly list, press Start, listen and type. You can hear the word again, hear it slowly, or reveal it, which counts as a miss.

Speech uses the voices already installed on your device through the Web Speech API. Some browsers use online voices, which would send the word to the voice provider; choose a local voice in your system settings if that matters. The tool needs a browser with speech synthesis, otherwise use the reveal button.

How to use it

  1. Paste your word list, one word per line.
  2. Press Start and listen.
  3. Type the spelling and press Enter.
  4. Read your score and the words to practise.

Frequently asked questions

Can it read other languages?

It uses the page language and your installed voices.

Is the word list stored?

No.

Why is there no sound?

Your browser may lack a voice. Use Show the word instead.

Does it check capitals?

No, case is ignored.
