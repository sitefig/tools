LinkedIn Text Formatter: Bold and Italic with Markers

Type **bold**, *italic* or ***both*** in your draft and get Unicode bold and italic text you can paste into LinkedIn, with an accessibility warning.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

LinkedIn posts take plain text only, so bold and italic are made with special Unicode letters. Many tools do this by selecting text. Here you write markers: **bold**, *italic* and ***both***.

The tool maps letters A to Z and digits to the sans-serif bold and italic blocks of Unicode. These are not real formatting. Screen readers may read them poorly, search cannot find them and some fonts show boxes, so use them for a word or two, never for key content.

How to use it

  1. Write your post with **bold** and *italic* markers.
  2. Read the converted text.
  3. Copy it into LinkedIn.
  4. Check it in the preview before posting.

Frequently asked questions

Is it accessible?

Not fully. Screen readers may spell the letters or skip them. Use sparingly.

Why are accented letters unchanged?

Unicode has no styled forms for most accented letters.

Why no italic digits?

Unicode has no italic digits in this block, so they stay plain.
