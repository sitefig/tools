Video Contact Sheet Maker: A Grid of Frames with Timestamps

Samples frames at even intervals and arranges them in a grid image with timestamps, for a quick overview of a video.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

A contact sheet lets you scan a long recording at a glance, review a screen capture or send a preview. The frames are taken from the middle of equal segments, so the first and last seconds are not over represented.

The sheet is a PNG you download. No upload happens.

How to use it

  1. Choose the video.
  2. Set the number of columns, rows and the thumbnail width.
  3. Choose whether to draw timestamps.
  4. Press Process and download the PNG.

Frequently asked questions

How many frames can I include?

Up to 12 by 12.

Can I choose exact frames?

Use the Video Frame Grabber for chosen times.

Why is a frame blurry?

It may be a frame between keyframes during motion.

Is the sheet large?

Width times columns, so keep thumbnails modest for sharing.
