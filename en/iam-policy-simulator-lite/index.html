IAM Policy Simulator Lite: Is This Action Allowed?

Evaluates action and resource requests against one IAM identity policy and shows which statement decided each one.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Checking a policy by reading it is error prone once wildcards and Deny statements mix. This page applies the basic evaluation rule: an explicit Deny wins, then an Allow, otherwise the request is denied by default.

It models one identity policy only. Conditions are shown but not evaluated, so results with a Condition say review. Resource policies, boundaries and SCPs are out of scope. The AWS policy simulator is the real answer.

How to use it

  1. Paste the policy JSON.
  2. Write one request per line: action then resource ARN.
  3. Read the verdict and the matching statement.
  4. Confirm important cases in the AWS simulator.

Frequently asked questions

Are conditions evaluated?

No. A match with a Condition is reported as review.

Do wildcards work?

Yes, * and ? in actions and resources. Actions are case-insensitive, resources are case-sensitive.

What about NotAction?

It is supported for Allow and Deny statements.

Is the policy uploaded?

No.
