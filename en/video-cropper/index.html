Video Cropper: Crop to 9:16, 1:1, 4:5 or a Custom Ratio

Crops a video to a new aspect ratio, such as 9:16 for stories or 1:1 for feeds, at a position you set with two percentages.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

A drag box is hard to use with a keyboard, so you choose the ratio and move the crop with two number fields: horizontal and vertical position from 0 to 100. The crop is the largest area of that ratio that fits in the frame.

Nothing is uploaded. The file is decoded by your browser and re-encoded, so quality drops a little and the result is WebM (or MP4 where your browser can record it). It records in real time, so a 60 second clip takes about a minute and the tab must stay visible.

How to use it

  1. Choose the video.
  2. Pick a ratio or enter a custom one such as 2:1.
  3. Set the horizontal and vertical position of the crop.
  4. Process and download.

Frequently asked questions

Can I drag a box on the picture?

No. Position fields work with keyboard, touch and screen readers. Try 50 and 50 first, then adjust.

What size is the output?

The crop size in source pixels, rounded to even numbers.

Does it zoom?

The crop is not scaled. Use the Video Resizer after if you need a specific size.

Is audio kept?

Yes.
