DPIA Screening Questionnaire (Is a DPIA Needed?)

Applies the nine WP29/EDPB criteria and the GDPR Art. 35(3) cases to tell you whether a DPIA is likely needed.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

A data protection impact assessment is required when processing is likely to result in a high risk to people. The WP29 guidelines list nine criteria; processing that meets two or more will in most cases need a DPIA. Art. 35(3) names cases where one is always required, and each national authority publishes its own list.

Tick what applies and get a screening result with the reasoning to write down. It is a screening aid only. Your DPO or lawyer decides.

How to use it

  1. Describe the processing activity in your own notes.
  2. Tick each criterion that applies.
  3. Tick the Art. 35(3) and national list boxes if they apply.
  4. Read the result and record the reasoning.

Frequently asked questions

What if I score one criterion?

Review it with your DPO. A DPIA may still be needed, and writing down why or why not is good practice.

Where is the national list?

On your supervisory authority's website. This tool does not include any national list.

What happens after a DPIA?

If high risk remains that you cannot reduce, Art. 36 requires consulting the authority first.
