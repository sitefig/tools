Cookie Policy Generator

Create a cookie policy draft with a table of cookies grouped as necessary, preferences, analytics and marketing. It is a starting template and not legal advice.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

If your site sets cookies or uses similar storage, visitors are entitled to a clear explanation of what each one is for and how long it lasts. A good policy lists cookies by category, so people can see why a login cookie is different from an advertising cookie. Writing that table from nothing is tedious.

This generator produces a draft with sections that explain what cookies are, who is responsible, how to manage them and a table of cookie categories. You choose whether you use preference, analytics and marketing cookies, name the analytics tool and its cookies, and add the session cookie name. It is a template only and not legal advice, and the cookie names and lifetimes must match what your site really sets. Everything is generated locally.

How to use it

  1. Enter your company name, website and contact email.
  2. Tick the cookie types you use: preferences, analytics and marketing.
  3. Fill in the analytics tool, the marketing tool and your session cookie details.
  4. Run the tool, compare the table with the cookies your site really sets, and have the text reviewed.

Frequently asked questions

Is this cookie policy legally compliant?

It is a generic starting template, not legal advice. Compliance depends on what your site really does and on local law, so have a qualified lawyer review the final text.

Does the policy create a consent banner?

No. It only creates the policy text. For the banner use the cookie consent banner generator, and make sure scripts really wait for consent before they run.

How do I find out which cookies my site sets?

Open browser developer tools and look at the Application or Storage tab, or run a HAR capture through the HAR analyzer. Update the table whenever you add or remove a service.

Does my data leave the browser?

No. The text is generated locally in your browser from the details you enter, and nothing is uploaded or stored on this site or sent to a third party.
