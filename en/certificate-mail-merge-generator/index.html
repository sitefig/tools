Certificate Generator: Mail-Merge Names into Printable Certificates

Creates one landscape certificate page per recipient from a list, with your wording, signature line and an optional background image.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Give a list of names, or a CSV with columns like name,course,date, and write the text under the name with placeholders such as {{course}}. Each person gets a page with a title, a name line, your wording, a signature line and a date.

You can add your own background image (PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP up to 6 MB), which is embedded in the page and not uploaded anywhere. A framed text area keeps the writing readable on busy artwork. Print all pages or choose single pages in the print dialog; save as PDF from there.

How to use it

  1. Paste names or a CSV with a name column.
  2. Write the title, wording and signature label.
  3. Optionally choose a background image.
  4. Print the pages or save as PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Does it send files anywhere?

No. Names and image stay in your browser.

One PDF per person?

It makes one page per person in one document. Print selected pages to separate PDFs.

Can I choose a template?

Use a background image for your design.

Which paper sizes?

A4 and Letter, landscape.
