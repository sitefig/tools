Third-Party Domain Classifier (Vendor and Category)

Matches domains against a built-in list of about 150 well-known vendors and groups them by category.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

A privacy review often starts with a list of domains from a network log, a sitemap of embeds or a scanner export. The next step is working out who each domain belongs to and what it does.

This tool matches each name (including subdomains) against a built-in list of common analytics, advertising, social, session replay, fonts, chat and CDN domains. Anything else is listed as unclassified for you to research. The list is not a full tracker database and it cannot tell what a request actually sends.

How to use it

  1. Paste domains or full URLs, one per line.
  2. Run the tool.
  3. Read the groups and the unclassified list.
  4. Research the unclassified domains and add vendors to your policy.

Frequently asked questions

How big is the list?

About 150 well-known domains. It is a starting point, not a database.

Can it see what data is sent?

No. It only classifies names.

What about first-party domains?

They show as unclassified. Remove your own domains before pasting.
