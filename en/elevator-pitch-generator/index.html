Elevator Pitch Generator From Problem, Solution and Audience

Builds pitch drafts in a problem-first and a positioning-statement structure from a few fields.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

A short pitch states who you help, what hurts, what you do and why it is different. Writing each part separately makes gaps obvious: if you cannot fill the problem, the pitch will not land.

The tool fills two templates and shows a word count and speaking time at a relaxed pace. These are drafts to edit by ear, not finished copy. It does not use AI.

How to use it

  1. Fill in who, the problem and how you solve it.
  2. Add what is different, proof and an ask if you have them.
  3. Read the two drafts aloud and edit them.

Frequently asked questions

How long should a pitch be?

Around 30 seconds, about 75 words, is typical.

Does it use AI?

No. It fills templates with your words.

What is a positioning statement?

A format: for a target who has a need, the product is a category that gives a benefit, unlike alternatives.
