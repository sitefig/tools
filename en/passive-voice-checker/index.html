Passive Voice and Long Sentence Checker

Flags sentences that look passive and sentences over a word limit, shows how many there are and the average sentence length.

Use the tool

Why this tool exists

Passive sentences and very long sentences make text harder to follow. Counting them gives a quick sense of where to tighten.

It is a heuristic, not a grammar parser. It looks for a form of "to be" followed by a past participle, so it finds most simple passives but also flags some adjectives, and it misses others. Passive voice is not wrong: it is useful when the doer is unknown or unimportant. There is no search engine rule on sentence length.

How to use it

  1. Paste your text.
  2. Set what counts as a long sentence.
  3. Read the counts.
  4. Rewrite flagged sentences where it helps the reader.

Frequently asked questions

Is passive voice bad for SEO?

No search engine penalty is known. It is a style issue.

Why is "She is tired" not flagged?

The tool skips a few common adjective cases but not all of them.

What is a good sentence length?

There is no fixed rule. Many style guides suggest an average under about 20 words.

Does it work in other languages?

No, English only.
